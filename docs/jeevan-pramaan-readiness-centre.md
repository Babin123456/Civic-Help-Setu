# Jeevan Pramaan and Pension Life-Certificate Readiness Centre

This document details the pension pathway classifications, submission options (FaceRD, biometric, doorstep), statutory submission windows, and error troubleshooting for Civic Help Setu's Pension Readiness Centre.

## Problem Statement
Every year, over 7 million Central, Defence, State, and EPS-95 pensioners across India must submit an annual Life Certificate (Jeevan Pramaan / DLC) to prevent pension disbursal stoppage. Common citizen friction includes:
1. **Window Confusion**: Super-senior pensioners (aged 80+) are legally entitled to submit from **1st October**, while others submit between **1st November and 30th November**. EPS-95 pensioners follow a rolling 12-month validity.
2. **SPARSH Migration Hurdles**: Defence veterans migrated to the SPARSH system often select their legacy disbursing bank instead of "SPARSH (PCDA Defence)", causing rejected life certificates.
3. **Biometric Failures**: Elderly pensioners frequently face fingerprint biometric degradation, but are unaware of free smartphone-based Face Authentication (Aadhaar FaceRD) or doorstep postal delivery.

## Pension Pathways Comparison

| Pathway | Disbursing Authority | Annual Submission Window | Tracking Portal | Special Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Central Civil** | CPAO / Banks | 1st Nov – 30th Nov (1st Oct for 80+) | `cpao.nic.in` | 12-digit Central PPO |
| **Defence (SPARSH)** | PCDA (P) Prayagraj | Birth month or Nov cycle | `sparsh.defencepension.gov.in` | Must select PDA as SPARSH |
| **State Government** | State Treasuries | 1st Nov – 30th Nov | State e-Treasury | Treasury code & bank account |
| **EPS-95 (EPFO)** | EPFO | Rolling 12 months from last submission | `epfindia.gov.in` | 12-digit EPS PPO & UAN |

## Submission Modes

```
  1. Digital FaceRD Mobile App (Free)
     └── Android/iPhone camera scan; no external biometric device needed.
  2. Biometric Iris / Fingerprint Scanner
     └── External RD service connected to PC or phone.
  3. India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) Doorstep Service
     └── Postman visits residence with micro-ATM for nominal ₹50 fee.
  4. Citizen Service Centres (CSC / MeeSeva)
     └── Local kiosk assisted biometric generation.
  5. Bank Branch Counter (Physical Form)
     └── Traditional signed physical certificate submitted at pension branch.
```

## Security & Privacy Non-Negotiables
- **Zero Credential Collection**: Civic Help Setu never requests, inputs, or logs Aadhaar numbers, biometric fingerprints, face captures, or banking passwords.
- **Family Helper Safety**: The readiness tool allows children and caregivers to prepare document checklists without handling sensitive credentials.
