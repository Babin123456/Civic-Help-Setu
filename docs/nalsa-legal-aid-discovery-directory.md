# National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) Legal-Aid Discovery Directory

## Statutory Framework
The **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Act No. 39 of 1987)** was enacted to constitute legal services authorities across India to provide free and competent legal services to the weaker sections of society, to ensure that opportunities for securing justice are not denied to any citizen by reason of economic or other disabilities, and to organize Lok Adalats to promote justice on a basis of equal opportunity.

---

## Statutory Eligibility under Section 12
Every person who has to file or defend a case shall be entitled to legal services under this Act if that person is:

1. **Section 12(a)**: A member of a **Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe**.
2. **Section 12(b)**: A victim of **trafficking in human beings** or beggar as referred to in Article 23 of the Constitution, or victim of mass disaster, ethnic violence, caste atrocity, flood, drought, earthquake or industrial disaster.
3. **Section 12(c)**: A **woman** or a **child** (eligible regardless of financial income).
4. **Section 12(d)**: A person with **disability** as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act.
5. **Section 12(e)**: An **industrial workman**.
6. **Section 12(g)**: A person in **custody**, including custody in a protective home, juvenile home, or psychiatric hospital.
7. **Section 12(h)**: In receipt of annual income less than the amount notified by the State Government (typically ₹1,50,000 to ₹3,00,000/year).

```
National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) - Apex Level
             │
             ▼

             │
             ▼
District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) - District Court Complex
             │
             ▼
Taluk Legal Services Committee (TLSC) - Sub-Divisional Court Level
             │
             ▼
Legal Aid Clinics & Jail Front Offices
```

---

## Scope of Free Legal Services
- Provision of an advocate at State expense (Panel Lawyer / Legal Aid Defense Counsel System - LADCS).
- Payment of court fees, process fees, translation charges, and expenses of witnesses.
- Drafting of plaints, written statements, appeals, petitions, and legal representations.
- Pre-litigation mediation and Lok Adalat dispute settlement.

---

## How to Apply
1. **In-Person**: Visit the Front Office of the DLSA located at any District Court complex.
2. **Online**: Submit an application via the NALSA Legal Aid Portal at [nalsa.gov.in](https://nalsa.gov.in/).
3. **Tele-Law / Helpline**: Call the 24x7 National Legal Aid Toll-Free Helpline at **15100**.
