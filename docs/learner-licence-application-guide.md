# Learner Licence Application Guide with Test & Validity Dates — Research Notes

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** A Learner's Licence (LL) is a temporary driving permit issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) under Section 8 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 authorizing an individual to learn driving on public roads while accompanied by a fully licensed driver.
- **What It Proves:**
  - Official statutory permission to practice driving a specified class of vehicle (MCWOG, MCWG, LMV) on public thoroughfares.
- **Strict Legal Limitations:**
  - An LL holder **MUST display red 'L' plates** on the front and rear of the vehicle.
  - The vehicle **MUST be accompanied** by a person possessing a valid permanent Driving Licence who is in a position to control the vehicle immediately.
  - An LL is NOT a permanent Driving Licence and cannot be used for commercial driving.

---

## 2. Age Criteria by Vehicle Category

Under Section 4 of the Motor Vehicles Act:
1. **Motor Cycle Without Gear (MCWOG up to 50cc):** Minimum **16 years** of age (requires written parental/guardian consent in Form 2).
2. **Motor Cycle With Gear (MCWG) / Light Motor Vehicle (LMV - Car/Jeep):** Minimum **18 years** of age.
3. **Transport / Commercial Vehicle:** Minimum **20 years** of age (requires holding a permanent LMV licence for at least 1 year).

---

## 3. Statutory Validity & Next Milestones

- **Validity Duration:** Strictly **6 Months** from the date of issuance (cannot be extended; if expired, candidate must re-apply and clear the LL test again).
- **Earliest Driving Licence (DL) Application Date:** Exactly **30 days** after the date of LL issuance. (A permanent DL test cannot be taken before completing the mandatory 30-day learning period).
- **Latest DL Application Date:** Within **6 months** (before the LL expires).

---

## 4. Contactless Online Test vs. RTO Appointment

Under MoRTH digital reforms on the Sarathi portal:
- **Aadhaar-Authenticated Mode (Faceless / Contactless):** Applicants authenticate via Aadhaar OTP and take the 15-question computerised traffic signs/rules test **from home** using web camera facial recognition. No RTO visit required. LL can be downloaded instantly.
- **Non-Aadhaar Mode:** Applicant books an appointment and visits the physical RTO to take the computerised learner test at the testing center.
