# UP Public Handpump Repair, Water-Point Downtime and Maintenance Tracker

## Overview
In rural Uttar Pradesh, hundreds of thousands of **India Mark-II** deep-well handpumps serve as the primary source of safe drinking water for hamlets (*Majras*), schools, and Anganwadi centres.

Administered jointly by **Gram Panchayats (Panchayati Raj Department - `panchayatiraj.up.nic.in`)**, **UP Jal Nigam (Rural)**, and the **State Water and Sanitation Mission (Jal Jeevan Mission - SWSM)**, broken pumps frequently linger non-functional for months due to unclear repair responsibility.

This service delivers an automated diagnostic and SLA enforcement tracker to expedite repairs.

---

## Fault Categories & Statutory Repair SLAs

Under UP Government orders regarding rural drinking water maintenance:
1. **Mechanical Surface Breakdown (Handle / Chain / Spindle)**:
   - **SLA**: **48 Hours**.
   - **Funding & Authority**: Gram Panchayat Gaon Nidhi (15th Finance Commission untied grants) through the village handpump mechanic (*Gram Mistri*).
2. **Subsurface Cylinder / Plunger Rod / Washer Failure**:
   - **SLA**: **72 Hours**.
   - **Authority**: Block Mobile Handpump Repair Gang coordinated by the Assistant Development Officer (Panchayat).
3. **Cracked Masonry Platform & Drainage Stagnation**:
   - **SLA**: **5 Days (120 Hours)**.
   - Requires construction of a cement apron and soak pit to prevent contaminated surface runoff from contaminating the groundwater aquifer.
4. **Water Column Dried / Deep Re-boring Required**:
   - **SLA**: **30 Days**.
   - Requires survey, technical sanction, and drilling rig deployment by Executive Engineer, UP Jal Nigam.
5. **Water Quality Contamination (Iron / Turbidity / Sand)**:
   - **SLA**: **7 Days**.
   - Testing through Field Test Kits (FTK) by Jal Sakhis and water laboratory analysis.

## Escalation Matrix & Funding Norms
- **Village Level**: Gram Pradhan & Gram Vikas Adhikari (GVA). Empowered to sanction minor repairs up to ₹2,000 per pump without block approval.
- **Block Level**: Assistant Development Officer (Panchayat) / Block Development Officer (BDO). Deploys repair mechanics and inspects logbooks.
- **District Level**: District Panchayat Raj Officer (DPRO) / Chief Development Officer (CDO).
- **UP Jal Jeevan Mission Helpline**: Toll-Free **1800-180-5678** or **1076** (CM Helpline).

## Community Verification & Privacy Standards
- **Consensus Threshold**: A water crisis alert is raised when 3 or more independent household observations corroborate downtime in the same hamlet.
- **Strict Privacy**: No individual mobile phone numbers or personal financial identifiers are logged.


## Village Community Handpump Inspection Checklist
When examining a non-functional handpump during Gram Sabha or ward walks:
1. **Physical Sound Test**: If the handle operates freely without resistance or suction sound, the top chain or rod connecting pin has dislodged.
2. **Vacuum / Suction Test**: If the handle feels heavy but yields only air or spurts of muddy water, the leather cup washer has worn down or the foot-valve has choked with sand.
3. **Apron Cleanliness**: Check if the wastewater drain is clogged with buffalo manure or mud, causing stagnant pools that breed mosquito vectors and pollute groundwater.
4. **Water Quality Check**: Observe if water pumped in the morning is clear or turns yellow-brown upon standing in a bucket (indicating high iron concentration).
5. **Asset Marking**: Verify whether the official 11-digit Panchayat asset identifier is painted legibly on the pump head cylinder.
