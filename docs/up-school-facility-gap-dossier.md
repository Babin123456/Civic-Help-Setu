# UP School Basic-Facility Gap & Parent Follow-Up Dossier

## Statutory Framework & Policy Mandates
This service empowers parents, School Management Committees (SMC), and village communities to systematically document and follow up on infrastructure gaps in Uttar Pradesh basic education institutions (**Parishadiya Prathmik & Uchha Prathmik Vidyalayas**) under:
1. **Right to Education (RTE) Act, 2009 (Schedule Norms for School Facilities)**: Mandating all-weather safe buildings, separate toilets for boys and girls, safe drinking water, barrier-free access, and boundary walls.
2. **Operation Kayakalp (UP Basic Shiksha Parishad)**: State flagship initiative tracking 19 mandatory school saturation parameters across all state government schools.
3. **Supreme Court of India Directives**: In *Environmental & Consumer Protection Foundation v. UOI (2012)*, mandating basic water and sanitation infrastructure across all government and aided schools.

---

## Operation Kayakalp 19-Parameter Saturation Checklist
The Uttar Pradesh Basic Shiksha Parishad evaluates primary school saturation across 19 physical indicators:
- Functional piped water connection & multi-tap handwashing stations.
- Tiled sanitary boys and girls toilet blocks with running water.
- Disabled-friendly ramp access with dual continuous handrails.
- All-weather secure pucca boundary walls and steel entrance gates.
- Metered electrical electrification with fans and safety earthing.

---

## 11-Digit UDISE+ Standard School Identification
- Every government school in Uttar Pradesh carries a unique 11-digit Unified District Information System for Education (UDISE+) code.
- Format: `[State: 09][District: 2 digits][Block: 2 digits][Village/Ward: 3 digits][School: 2 digits]`.
- Enforces unambiguous institutional identification before attaching parent observations.

---

## Non-Negotiable Child & Educator Safeguards
1. **Zero Child Data Collection**: No student names, roll numbers, photographs of children, or class attendance data are ever requested or stored.
2. **Infrastructure Focus (No Staff Allegations)**: The dossier strictly tracks physical amenities (water taps, toilet doors, roof integrity, ramps) and rejects personal complaints targeting named teachers or staff members.
3. **Community vs. Official Record Distinction**: Community observations are transparently badged as *Community Observations* and are never presented as official statutory inspection audits.
4. **Minimum Aggregation Threshold**: Public trend analytics require a minimum of **3 independent observations** to prevent individual targeting or selective bias.

---

## Administrative Follow-Up Roadmap
- **Level 1: School Management Committee (SMC)** — Presented during monthly parent-teacher meetings for local repair funds utilization.
- **Level 2: Block Education Officer (BEO)** — Formal submission for infrastructure allocation under Operation Kayakalp gram panchayat funds.
- **Level 3: District Basic Shiksha Adhikari (BSA) & CM Helpline (1076)** — Departmental escalation if unaddressed after 30 working days.
