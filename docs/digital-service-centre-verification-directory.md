# Public Digital Service Centre Verification & Visit Feedback Directory

## Scope & Purpose
Public digital service kiosks — including **Common Service Centres (CSC 2.0 / 3.0)**, State Delivery Centres (**e-Mitra, MeeSeva, Maha e-Seva, Banglar Sahayata Kendra**), and **Post Office Seva Kendras** — deliver frontline civic and identity services to millions of citizens.

This directory ensures operational transparency, safeguards citizens from unauthorized middlemen or extortionate charges, and provides verified government rate references.

---

## Centre Classifications & Authorities

| Kiosk Network | Operating Model | Governing Authority | Max Permissible Service Charge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common Service Centres (CSC)** | Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) | CSC e-Gov / Ministry of Electronics & IT | ₹30 max facilitation |
| **State Portals (e-Mitra / MeeSeva)** | Notified District Franchisees | State IT / Electronic Delivery of Services | ₹25 max facilitation |
| **Post Office Citizen Centres** | Department of Posts Staff | India Post | ₹0 facilitation (Statutory fee only) |

---

## Official Government Service Fee Benchmarks

- **Mandatory Aadhaar Biometric Update (Ages 5 & 15)**: **₹0 (Free)**. No kiosk charge allowed.
- **Aadhaar Demographic Update (Name, Address, DOB)**: **₹50** (inclusive of GST).
- **Aadhaar Biometric Update (Adults)**: **₹100** (inclusive of GST).
- **PM-KISAN e-KYC (Biometric)**: Maximum ₹15 facilitation fee.
- **PAN Card Application (Form 49A)**: Government fee ₹107 + max ₹30 facilitation = ₹137.
- **Income / Caste / Domicile Certificate Submission**: Notified state fee (₹15–₹25) + max ₹30 facilitation.

## Technical and Accessibility Standards for Digital Centres
- **Hardware & Security**: STQC-certified fingerprint/iris biometric scanners with encrypted transmission conforming to UIDAI Aadhaar 2.0 specs.
- **Physical Accessibility**: Presence of entrance ramps and wheelchair access, prioritized for senior citizens and persons with disabilities.
- **National Helpdesk Support**: In case of server downtime or system failures, VLEs and citizens can reach the CSC Helpdesk at **14599** or UIDAI Resident Support at **1947**.

---

## Citizen Rights & Safeguards
1. **Computerized Receipt Mandate**: The operator must furnish an official printout / electronic receipt generated directly by the portal for every transaction. Handwritten chits are invalid.
2. **Mandatory Rate Chart Display**: Every kiosk is contractually bound to exhibit a legible rate chart approved by the District e-Governance Society (DeGS).
3. **Receipt Refusal & Overcharging Redressal**: If an operator refuses a receipt or charges more than the notified amount:
   - Note the **CSC ID / Kiosk ID**.
   - Lodge an immediate complaint on the **DeGS District Helpline** or **CPGRAMS**.
   - Repeated overcharging leads to blacklisting and deactivation of the VLE credential.
