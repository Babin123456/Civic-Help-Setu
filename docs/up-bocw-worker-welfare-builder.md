# UP Construction Worker Welfare Benefit and Renewal Journey Builder

## Overview
The **Uttar Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (UPBOCW / `upbocw.in`)** implements social security and welfare schemes under the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996.

This module provides a citizen-first journey builder for unorganized construction labourers, masons, painters, electricians, and plumbers across Uttar Pradesh to verify their statutory eligibility, manage annual renewals, and unlock statutory welfare entitlements.

---

## Statutory Rules & Eligibility

### 1. The 90-Day Rule
- **Mandatory Requirement**: Under Section 12 of the BOCW Act, an applicant must have worked for **at least 90 days** in building or construction work in the preceding 12 months.
- **Proof of 90 Days Work**:
  - Certificate from the building contractor / private employer.
  - Certificate from a registered trade union.
  - Certificate from the Gram Pradhan (for rural areas) or Ward Councillor (for urban local bodies).

### 2. Age Prescriptions
- The worker must be between **18 and 60 years** of age at the time of initial registration or renewal.

### 3. Non-Enrollment in Organized Social Security
- Workers must submit a self-declaration confirming they are not enrolled under EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund) or ESIC (Employees' State Insurance).

---

## Annual Registration Renewal & Grace Period

- **Annual Fee**: Statutory nominal fee of **₹20/year** (or ₹60 for 3 years).
- **Grace Period**: 90 days from the date of expiry.
  - Within 90 days: Can be renewed online via CSC or the UPBOCW citizen portal without losing continuity.
  - After 90 days: Marked as `LAPSED`. Requires physical appearance and verification before the Assistant Labour Commissioner (ALC).

---

## Major Welfare Schemes Supported

1. **Sant Ravidas Shiksha Protsahan Yojana**:
   - Monthly educational stipend (₹100 to ₹5,000) for school/college-going children of registered workers.
2. **Matritva, Shishu Evam Balika Madad Yojana**:
   - Maternity grant (₹20,000–₹25,000) and newborn care support.
3. **Kanya Vivah Sahayata Yojana**:
   - Marriage grant of ₹55,000 per daughter (up to 2 daughters) or ₹61,000 during mass marriage events.
4. **Tool Kit Vitran Sahayata**:
   - Modern hand tools or tool subsidy (up to ₹5,000) matching the registered trade.

---

## Privacy & Security Architecture
- **No Aadhaar Harvesting**: No 12-digit Aadhaar credentials, iris scans, or fingerprint templates are collected.
- **Masked Identity**: Registration numbers and bank accounts are masked (`UPBOCW-****-XXXX`).
- **Offline Representation Ready**: Dossiers can be printed and presented during public hearings (Shramik Suvidha Kendra / Tehsil Diwas).
