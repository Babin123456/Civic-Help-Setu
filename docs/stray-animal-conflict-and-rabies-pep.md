# Municipal Stray Animal Conflict, Animal Birth Control (ABC) Rules 2023 Audit & Rabies PEP Emergency Workspace

## 1. Overview & Statutory Context

Urban residents across Indian municipalities encounter escalating human-street dog conflicts, dog bite emergencies, and friction regarding animal feeding. This workspace bridges public safety and animal welfare through:
1. **The Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023** notified under Section 38 of the *Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (PCA) Act, 1960*.
2. **National Rabies Control Programme (NRCP) Guidelines** and World Health Organization (WHO) Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) protocols.
3. Landmark Supreme Court directives (*Animal Welfare Board of India v. A. Nagaraja (2014) 7 SCC 547*) strictly prohibiting the unlawful relocation of street dogs.

## 2. Emergency Rabies Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) Engine

Rabies is virtually 100% fatal once clinical encephalitic or paralytic symptoms manifest, but **100% preventable** when appropriate PEP is administered promptly.

### 2.1 WHO / NRCP Contact Categories
- **Category I (No Exposure):** Touching or feeding animals, animal licks on intact skin.
  - *Action:* Thoroughly wash exposed skin surfaces with soap and water. No vaccine or RIG indicated.
- **Category II (Minor Exposure):** Nibbling of uncovered skin, minor scratches or abrasions without bleeding.
  - *Action:* Immediate thorough wound washing with soap and running water for $\ge 15$ minutes. Immediate antiseptic application (povidone-iodine). Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV) series mandatory. RIG not indicated in immunocompetent individuals.
- **Category III (Severe Exposure):** Single or multiple transdermal bites or scratches, licks on broken skin, mucous membrane contamination with saliva from licks, exposures to bats or wild animals.
  - *Action:* Immediate 15-minute wound washing, antiseptic application, Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV) series mandatory **AND** Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG) local wound infiltration mandatory.

### 2.2 Approved ARV Vaccination Regimens
- **Essen Regimen (Intramuscular - IM):**
  - 5 Doses: Days 0, 3, 7, 14, and 28.
  - Injected in deltoid muscle (adults/children $\ge 2$ years) or anterolateral thigh (infants). Gluteal injection is strictly contraindicated due to variable fat depth and reduced immunogenicity.
- **Thai Red Cross Regimen (Intradermal - ID):**
  - 2 Sites (0.1 mL each) on Days 0, 3, 7, and 28 (4 clinic visits).
  - Dose-sparing, cost-effective regimen approved by WHO and Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India.
- **Zagreb Regimen (Intramuscular - IM):**
  - 2-1-1 regimen: 2 doses on Day 0 (bilateral deltoids), 1 dose on Day 7, 1 dose on Day 21.

### 2.3 Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG) Weight-Based Dosage
- **Human Rabies Immunoglobulin (HRIG):** $20\text{ IU/kg}$ body weight ($150\text{ IU/mL}$ formulation).
- **Equine Rabies Immunoglobulin (ERIG):** $40\text{ IU/kg}$ body weight ($300\text{ IU/mL}$ formulation).
- *Infiltration Guideline:* Infiltrate as much calculated dose as anatomically possible directly into and around all bite wounds. Any remaining volume is injected IM at a site distant from the vaccine site.

## 3. Animal Birth Control (ABC) Rules, 2023 Ward Auditor

Under Rule 11 of the ABC Rules 2023, local municipal authorities (Municipal Corporations and Municipal Councils) are statutorily mandated to execute systematic ABC drives:
1. **Humane Capture (Rule 11(4)):** Butterfly nets and sacking only; capture using wire loops, iron tongs, or brutal methods is illegal.
2. **Pre-Operative Care (Rule 11(7)):** Pups below 6 months, pregnant females, and sick animals are exempt from surgery until healthy.
3. **Right-Ear "V-Notch" (Rule 11(9)):** Universal visible mark created during surgery while animal is under general anesthesia.
4. **Anti-Rabies Vaccination (Rule 11(10)):** Mandatory ARV administered during the surgical procedure.
5. **Post-Operative Recovery (Rule 11(11)):** 3–4 days kennel hospitalization with food and water until healing.
6. **Mandatory Same-Territory Return (Rule 11(16)):** Animals **must be released back into the exact same locality/street** from which they were caught.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Prohibition Against Dog Relocation:** Relocating community dogs is a criminal offense under Section 11 of the *Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960* and violates Supreme Court directives. Relocation creates vacuum zones, induces panic and biting aggression in neighboring packs, and dismantles rabies herd immunity.

## 4. Community Feeder Regulatory Charter (Rule 20)

Under Rule 20 of the ABC Rules 2023:
- **Demarcated Spots:** Municipalities and RWAs must mutually designate specific feeding spots away from children play areas, building staircases, and main entry/exit gates.
- **Fixed Hours:** Low pedestrian traffic hours (e.g., 5:30 AM–7:00 AM and 10:30 PM–11:30 PM).
- **Caretaker Legal Immunity:** RWAs, housing societies, or security guards cannot harass, intimidate, or fine animal feeders. Compassion toward living creatures is protected under Article 51A(g) of the Constitution of India.
- **Civic Duties:** Feeders must maintain spot cleanliness, remove empty food bowls, and coordinate with municipal veterinary staff for 100% sterilization and vaccination.
