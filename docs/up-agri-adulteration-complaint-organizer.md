# UP Agricultural Input Adulteration Complaint Evidence Organizer

## Overview
Sub-standard, spurious, or adulterated agricultural inputs—counterfeit **hybrid seeds**, fake **DAP / Urea fertilizers** mixed with clay or rock phosphate, and diluted **chemical pesticides**—cause devastating seasonal crop failures for farmers across Uttar Pradesh.

Regulated strictly under:
1. **Seeds Act, 1966 & Seeds Rules, 1968**
2. **Fertilizer (Control) Order, 1985 (FCO)** & the **Essential Commodities Act, 1955**
3. **Insecticides Act, 1968**
4. **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**

This service guides farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) through building a legally robust, evidence-backed complaint for statutory laboratory testing, stop-sale enforcement, and consumer compensation.

## 3 Core Statutory Input Categories

| Category | Statutory Inspection Authority | Forensic Quality Testing Laboratory | Legal Sampling Mandate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seeds (Beej)** | Seed Inspector / SDAO | State Seed Testing Laboratory (SSTL) | 3 identical sealed packets testing germination % & genetic purity. |
| **Fertilizers (Khad)** | Fertilizer Inspector / DAO | Regional Fertilizer Quality Control Lab (FQCL) | 3 poly-sealed samples taken with a stainless-steel sampling probe/spear. |
| **Pesticides (Keetnashak)** | Insecticide Inspector | State Pesticide Testing Laboratory (SPTL) | Tested for active ingredient (AI) concentration against CIBRC registration. |

## Essential Evidentiary Suite for Farmers

Without specific statutory attachments, administrative complaints before the District Agriculture Officer (DAO) or District Consumer Forum fail:
1. **Authorized Cash Memo / e-POS Bill**: Proof of purchase with dealer license number, signature, and GSTIN.
2. **Original Packaging**: Empty bag or container clearly exhibiting the printed Batch Number, Lot Number, Manufacturing Date, and Expiry Date.
3. **Sealed Leftover Portion**: Retaining at least 250–500 grams in a clean, airtight polythene pouch for chemical analysis.
4. **Field Photographic Evidence**: Geo-tagged photographs showing patchy/zero seedling emergence or crop burning.
5. **Land Holding Proof**: Certified Khatauni extract from `bhulekh.up.gov.in` substantiating the acreage treated.

## Statutory Remedies & Punitive Measures
- **Stop-Sale Order (Vikray Pratibandh)**: The District Agriculture Officer is empowered to freeze all retail inventory of the defective batch across the district.
- **FIR under Section 3/7 of Essential Commodities Act**: Unlawful manufacturing or sale of adulterated fertilizer attracts non-bailable criminal prosecution.
- **Crop Loss Compensation**: Filing before the **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (DCDRC)** for recovery of input purchase costs and estimated harvest yield losses.
- **Kisan Call Centre Toll-Free**: **1800-180-1551**.


## Field Guidance for Agricultural Vigilance
1. **Pre-Purchase Precautions**:
   - Always purchase seeds, fertilizers, and insecticides from authorized cooperative societies (IFFCO/KRIBHCO/PACCS) or licensed private input dealers displaying active UP Agriculture Department licenses.
   - Demand a computer-printed e-POS slip and compare the printed batch number against the tag on the bag.
2. **Immediate Reaction to Crop Failure**:
   - If seedlings fail to germinate within 7–10 days of sowing, do NOT plow the field immediately.
   - Take date-stamped digital photographs of the field and inform the village Kisan Sahayak or Agriculture Technical Assistant (BTA) to record a spot inspection memorandum (*Mauka Punchnama*).
3. **Sealing the Evidentiary Sample**:
   - Retain 250 grams of seeds or fertilizer from the opened bag in a clean glass jar or sealed polythene bag witnessed by two neighboring farmers.


## Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission Filing Checklist
When seeking judicial compensation for total or partial harvest loss before the District Consumer Commission:
1. **Notice to Vendor**: Send a formal registered notice to the retail dealer and seed/chemical manufacturer detailing the loss and demanding compensation within 15 days.
2. **Joint Inspection Panchnama**: Attach the field verification certificate signed by the Block Agriculture Officer or revenue Lekhpal.
3. **Receipt and Invoice Proof**: Submit the attested copy of the purchase invoice.
4. **Certified Seed Testing Report**: File the analysis report issued by the government quality control laboratory.
5. **Acreage & Yield Loss Calculation**: Estimate loss based on the average district yield published by the Directorate of Agricultural Statistics and Crop Insurance.
