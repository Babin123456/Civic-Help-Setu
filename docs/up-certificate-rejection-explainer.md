# UP Caste, Income, and Residence Certificate Rejection-Reason Explainer

## Overview
Under the **Uttar Pradesh Right to Public Services Act (Janhit Guarantee Adhiniyam, 2011)** and the UP Revenue Code, 2006, citizens frequently apply for statutory certificates through **e-District UP (`edistrict.up.gov.in`)**.

However, thousands of applications face opaque or shorthand rejection remarks recorded by village revenue accountants (*Lekhpal*), Revenue Inspectors (*Kanoongo*), or *Tehsildars*.

This module breaks down obscure rejection notices into clear legal explanations, checklists of acceptable documentary substitutes, and formal First Appeal pathways.

---

## Supported Revenue Certificates

1. **Caste Certificate (Jati Praman Patra)**:
   - For SC, ST, and OBC communities.
   - Statutory disposal time: **15 working days**.
   - **Crucial Rule**: In Uttar Pradesh, caste is recognized strictly according to **paternal lineage (Pita ke paksh ka praman)**. Maternal or marital documents alone do not suffice.

2. **Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra)**:
   - Evaluates household gross income including agricultural holdings (Khatauni RoR) and formal/informal trade earnings.
   - Statutory disposal time: **15 working days**.

3. **Domicile / Residence Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra)**:
   - Proves continuous domicile in Uttar Pradesh (minimum 3 years).
   - Statutory disposal time: **20 working days**.

## Frequent Objection Types & Practical Solutions

| Rejection Remark Pattern | Core Administrative Ground | Concrete Actionable Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| *Khatauni mismatch / Bhoomi vivad* | Land record contradicts reported income or lineage. | Download certified Khatauni RoR from `bhulekh.up.gov.in` and provide joint family pedigree (*Vanshavali*). |
| *Pita ke paksh ka praman nahi* | Maternal or marital proof submitted without paternal proof. | Attach father's, paternal grandfather's, or real uncle's school TC, land deed, or older caste cert. |
| *Mauke par upastithi nahi / Kirayedar* | Lekhpal physical spot check did not locate applicant. | Obtain Gram Pradhan or Nagar Palika Councillor certificate verifying continuous physical stay. |
| *Shapath patra apurna* | Mandatory affidavit lacks notary stamp or court fee. | Download standard Swaghoshit format, affix ₹10 non-judicial e-stamp/notary attestation, and re-scan under 100 KB. |

## Statutory Appeal Pathway (Janhit Guarantee Adhiniyam)

When an application is rejected arbitrarily or delayed beyond the guaranteed time frame:
1. **First Appellate Authority**: Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Tehsildar (Appeals). Must decide within 30 days.
2. **Second Appellate Authority**: District Magistrate (DM) / Additional District Magistrate (ADM Revenue).
3. **Draft Appeal Generator**: The system formats a ready-to-print Memorandum of Appeal citing Section 5(1) of the UP Janhit Guarantee Adhiniyam, 2011.

## Sampoorna Samadhan Diwas (Tehsil Diwas) Strategy
For citizens whose appeals or re-applications face persistent delays, the Government of Uttar Pradesh convenes **Sampoorna Samadhan Diwas** on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of every month.

### Action Plan at Tehsil Diwas:
1. **Attendance**: Reach the Tehsil auditorium by 09:30 AM before the bench presided over by the District Magistrate (DM) / SDM.
2. **Grievance Entry**: Register the complaint at the automated IGRS / e-District kiosk to get an official computerized slip.
3. **Submission**: Present the printed *Memorandum of Appeal* generated by this service, attaching the previous rejection screenshot and paternal lineage / Khatauni evidence.
4. **Disposal**: Under UP Government orders, matters presented in Tehsil Diwas carry a strict 7-day disposal and physical re-inquiry mandate.
