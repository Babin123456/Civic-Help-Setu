# Legal Aid Eligibility and District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) Finder

## Context & Objectives
Under Article 39A of the Constitution of India and the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, free legal aid is a fundamental statutory entitlement for vulnerable groups. However, citizens often do not know which legal aid authority to approach, whether they qualify, or what documents to bring.

## Key System Components
1. **Statutory Section 12 Criteria Matrix (`legalAidDirectoryData.js`)**:
   - Outlines all categories entitled to free legal aid: women, children, SC/ST, disability, under-trials, and low-income citizens.
   - Highlights categories exempt from income caps under Section 12(a)-(g).
2. **DLSA & SLSA Directory (`legalAidFinderService.js`)**:
   - Verified office locations, helplines (15100 NALSA National Helpline), and Lok Adalat pre-litigation conciliation services.
3. **Appointment Preparation Checklist**:
   - Step-by-step guidance on gathering case summons, affidavits, and identity papers without offering legal opinions or predicting judicial outcomes.
4. **Emergency Safety Signposting**:
   - Prominently directs urgent domestic violence or child abuse complaints to emergency police (112) and women's lines (181).
