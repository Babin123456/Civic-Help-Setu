# Street-Vendor Certificate and Vending-Zone Application Workspace

## Statutory Framework
The **Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014** protects the livelihood rights of street vendors across India, regulates street vending through Town Vending Committees (TVC), and establishes grievance redressal mechanisms.

### Core Statutory Mandates
1. **Section 3(1) - Town Vending Committee Survey**:
   The TVC conducts a periodic survey of all existing street vendors within its jurisdiction at least once every 5 years.
2. **Section 3(3) - Protection from Eviction**:
   No street vendor shall be evicted or relocated until the survey is completed and a Certificate of Vending (CoV) has been issued.
3. **Section 4 - Issuance of Certificate of Vending**:
   Every street vendor identified under the survey shall be granted a certificate of vending subject to reasonable terms and conditions.

## Separation: Certificate of Vending (CoV) vs. PM SVANidhi

| Dimension | Certificate of Vending (CoV) | PM SVANidhi Scheme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enactment** | Street Vendors Act, 2014 | Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA) Scheme |
| **Nature** | Statutory operational licence & identity protection | Collateral-free working capital micro-credit |
| **Authority** | Town Vending Committee (TVC) / Municipal Corporation | Scheduled Banks / MFIs / NBFCs |
| **Financial Loan**| No loan component | ₹10,000 / ₹20,000 / ₹50,000 tranches |
| **Prerequisites** | Residence, Survey token, Undertaking | CoV or TVC Letter of Recommendation (LoR) |

## Vending Classifications & Demarcated Zones

### Categories of Vendors
- **Stationary Vendor**: Operates at a specific fixed location demarcated in the vending plan.
- **Mobile Vendor**: Vends on foot, pushcart, or cycle moving through designated sectors.
- **Peripatetic Vendor**: Operates in weekly markets (haats), night bazaars, or festival grounds.

### Designated Zones
- **Vending Zone**: Demarcated area open for commercial vending under TVC regulations.
- **Restricted Vending Zone**: Vending permitted during specific time bands or under seasonal caps.
- **No-Vending Zone**: Strictly prohibited areas based on traffic safety, heritage, or statutory master plans.

## Mandatory Documentation Checklist
- Proof of Identity (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Ration Card)
- Proof of Local Residence within Urban Local Body (ULB)
- Passport-size photographs and geo-tagged stall/cart photograph
- Statutory self-declaration undertaking affirming no other commercial premises held
- TVC survey slip or token (if surveyed)
