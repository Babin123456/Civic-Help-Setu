# Land Record Record-of-Rights (RoR) & Certified Extract Guide (India)

## Statutory Context
- Governed by respective State Land Revenue Codes (e.g., **Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966**, **UP Revenue Code, 2006**, **Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964**, **Tamil Nadu Patta Passbook Act, 1983**).
- Digital Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP), Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India.
- Managed through State Land Records (Bhulekh / Bhoomi / AnyRoR / BanglarBhumi / Tamil Nilam) portals.

## State-Specific Nomenclature & Legal Meaning
1. **7/12 Extract (Saat-Baara - Maharashtra & Gujarat)**:
   - Village Form VII (Gaon Namuna 7): Land rights, owner/khatadar names, occupancy type (Class 1 / Class 2), total area, encumbrances/pot-kharab, loan liabilities.
   - Village Form XII (Gaon Namuna 12): Crop inspection register (Pik Pahani), irrigated/unirrigated status, tree cultivation.
   - Form 8A: Khata register detailing tax/revenue assessment and liability.
2. **Khatauni & Khasra (Uttar Pradesh, MP, Bihar, Uttarakhand - UP Bhulekh)**:
   - **Khasra**: Spatial parcel identifier detailing parcel geometry, soil classification, actual cultivator, and boundary details.
   - **Khatauni**: Register of Record of Rights listing tenure-holders (Bhumidhar with transferable rights, Bhumidhar with non-transferable rights, Asami), shareholding ratio, and ongoing litigation/stay orders.
3. **Patta, Chitta & FMB (Tamil Nadu - AnyQuery / AnyService / Tamil Nilam)**:
   - **Patta**: Revenue record proving land ownership issued by the Tahsildar.
   - **Chitta**: Document describing classification of land (wet/Nanjai, dry/Punjai) and tax assessment.
   - **Field Measurement Book (FMB)**: Sketch/survey map delineating individual sub-divisions and boundary stone coordinates.
4. **Jamabandi & Nakal (Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh - PLRS / Jamabandi Portal)**:
   - Title register published every 4 years updating owner names, Khewat/Khatauni numbers, Khasra numbers, and mortgages/encumbrances.
5. **RTC / Pahani (Karnataka - Bhoomi Portal)**:
   - Record of Rights, Tenancy, and Crops (RTC) detailing Survey Number, Hissa Number, Khata Number, land type, and owner particulars.

## Critical Distinction: Informational View vs. Certified Extract
- **Informational / Unsigned Web Copy**: Free to view; meant solely for citizen awareness, verifying survey details, or initial due diligence. Clearly stamped with "Not Valid for Legal / Court Purpose".
- **Digitally Signed / Certified Extract**: Authenticated with a cryptographic signature, QR code, or wet seal from the Talathi/Tahsildar. Legally admissible in courts, accepted by banks for mortgage/crop loans, and required for property registry registration.
