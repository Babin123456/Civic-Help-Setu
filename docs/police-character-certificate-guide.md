# State Police Character Certificate Guide for Employment and Tenancy (India)

## Statutory Context
- Governed by respective **State Police Acts** (e.g. **Delhi Police Act, 1978**, **Maharashtra Police Act, 1951**, **Uttar Pradesh Police Regulations**).
- Delivered digitally under the Ministry of Home Affairs' **Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems (CCTNS)** initiative.

## Purpose & Scope
- Issued by the local **District Police Commissionerate / Superintendent of Police (SP)**.
- Intended strictly for:
  - Private sector employment background checks (IT, finance, manufacturing).
  - Private security guards and armed cash-van personnel.
  - School bus drivers, conductors, and attendant staff.
  - Commercial tenancy and domestic worker verification.
  - Government contract tenders and excise / arms shop license applications.
- **Critical Distinction**: This certificate is purely a domestic verification report. It is **NOT valid for international visas, passport applications, or foreign emigration** (which exclusively require an MEA Passport Seva PCC).

## State Variations & Portals
- **Delhi Police**: `delhipolice.gov.in` (PCC module) - Fee ₹250; completely faceless digital PDF with QR code.
- **Uttar Pradesh (UPCOP)**: `uppolice.gov.in` - UPCOP mobile app - Fee ₹50; automated beat officer inquiry.
- **Maharashtra Police**: `aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in` / `mahapolice.gov.in` - Police Clearance for Character verification.
- **Karnataka Police (KSP)**: `ksp.karnataka.gov.in` - Police Verification Certificate (PVC).
