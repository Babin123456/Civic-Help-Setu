# Local Government Public Meeting Minutes & Decision Archive

## Constitutional & Legal Framework
Decentralized governance in India is constitutionally established under the **73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts, 1992**, creating three tiers of local self-government:
1. **Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)**: Municipal Corporations, Municipal Councils, and Nagar Panchayats.
2. **Ward Committees & Area Sabhas**: Grassroots citizen representation within municipal wards.
3. **Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs)**: Zilla Parishad, Panchayat Samiti, and Gram Panchayat / Gram Sabha.

---

## Proactive Public Disclosure under RTI Act 2005
Under **Section 4(1)(b)(viii)** of the Right to Information Act, 2005, every local authority is legally mandated to proactively publish:
> *"A statement of the boards, councils, committees and other bodies consisting of two or more persons constituted as its part or for the purpose of its advice, and as to whether meetings of those boards, councils, committees and other bodies are open to the public, or the minutes of such meetings are accessible for public."*

---

## Meeting Stages & Resolution Enforceability

| Resolution Status | Legal Effect | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Draft Minutes** | Non-binding | Recorded by secretary; circulated for member confirmation. |
| **Confirmed Resolution** | Legally Operative | Formally confirmed in subsequent sitting; binding on executive. |
| **Referred Back** | Suspended | Sent back by Municipal Commissioner or State Govt for review. |
| **Disapproved / Rejected** | Ineffective | Defeated by majority vote. |

---

## Statutory Quorum Rules
A resolution passed without satisfying statutory quorum requirements is vulnerable to judicial quashing:
- **Municipal Corporation / General Body**: Typically **33% (one-third)** of total elected councillors.
- **Ward Committee**: Typically **20%** of nominated/resident members.
- **Gram Sabha**: Typically **10%** of registered adult voters of the village (with mandated representation of women and SC/ST electors).

---

## Citizen Participation & Transparency Safeguards
- **Advance Agenda Publishing**: Wards are required to publish meeting agendas at least 48 to 72 hours in advance on municipal portals and physical notice boards.
- **Citizen Question Hour**: Provision for residents of the ward/panchayat to ask questions regarding civic expenditures and pending works.
- **Certified Copy Accessibility**: Certified true copies of resolutions must be supplied within 7 working days upon formal application.

---

## Linking Resolutions to Executive Action
Minutes in this archive link directly to downstream executive manifestations:
- **Work Orders & Tenders**: Contracts issued to implement infrastructure resolutions.
- **Gazette Notifications**: Local bye-law amendments and property tax revisions.
- **Budget Allocations**: Expenditure line items approved in annual civic budgets.
