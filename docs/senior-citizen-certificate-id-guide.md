# Senior Citizen Certificate or ID Issuance Guide by State — Research Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#510 — Add a Senior Citizen Certificate or ID issuance guide by state](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/510)  
**Scope:** Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007, State-level Social Welfare department Senior Citizen ID card schemes, age thresholds (60+ years), travel/transport concessions, healthcare priority, and grievance cells.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** A Senior Citizen Certificate or Identity Card is an official state/UT document issued to individuals aged 60 years and above, confirming senior status within the jurisdiction.
- **Not a Single Uniform National ID:**
  - There is NO single compulsory nationwide "Senior Citizen Passport/Card" issued by the central government. Proof of age can often be established directly via Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Voter ID.
  - However, state-specific Senior Citizen Cards (e.g., Delhi e-District, Tamil Nadu Social Welfare, Maharashtra Senior Citizen Card) provide institutional recognition for state-run bus concessions, state hospital priority counters, Old Age Pensions (IGNOAPS / state schemes), and Maintenance Tribunal representations.

---

## 2. Who Can Apply & Age Criteria

- Any Indian citizen aged **60 years or above** on the date of application.
- Resident of the issuing State/UT for the minimum continuous period prescribed by the state social welfare rules (typically 1 to 5 years, or ordinary resident status).

---

## 3. Benefits and Concessions Attached

1. **Healthcare:** Dedicated priority OPD counters, geriatric wards, and free/discounted diagnostics in government hospitals.
2. **Public Transit:** State road transport corporation (SRTC) bus concessions (ranging from 25% to 50% discount or free travel depending on state rules).
3. **Banking & Taxation:** Higher interest rates on Senior Citizen Savings Schemes (SCSS) and Fixed Deposits; higher basic income tax exemption limits under Section 80TTB.
4. **Maintenance Tribunal Protection:** Fast-track legal recourse against neglect or eviction by children/heirs under the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007.

---

## 4. Required Documents Checklist

1. Proof of Date of Birth: Birth Certificate, 10th marksheet, Passport, PAN Card, or Voter ID.
2. Proof of Residence: Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill, Water Bill, or Registered Rent Deed in the state.
3. Passport-size photographs with blood group indication (where required for emergency cards).
4. Medical fitness certificate / blood group report from a registered medical practitioner.
