# Child Protection and Missing-Child Official Help Directory

## Context & Objectives
In urgent child safety or missing child crises, parents, relatives, and citizens require verified statutory channels without navigating commercial clutter, unverified social media forwards, or scam reward claims.

## Key Capabilities & Statutory Protections
1. **Emergency-First Routing (`childProtectionOfficialData.js`)**:
   - 1098 (Childline under Mission Vatsalya) and 112 (National Emergency Support System).
   - Supreme Court mandate (*Bachpan Bachao Andolan vs Union of India*) requiring immediate registration of FIRs for missing children.
2. **Verified District Child Protection Ecosystem (`childProtectionDirectoryService.js`)**:
   - Child Welfare Committees (CWC) - bench of magistrates with judicial powers.
   - District Child Protection Units (DCPU) and District Child Protection Officers (DCPO).
   - Special Juvenile Police Units (SJPU) in every police commissionerate.
3. **Strict Minor Identity Protection Safeguard (Section 74 JJ Act 2015)**:
   - Civic Help Setu strictly prohibits publishing names, photos, or identifying information of minors on public noticeboards.
4. **Official TrackChild and KhoyaPaya Portals**:
   - Direct links to official Ministry of Women and Child Development portals (https://trackchild.gov.in).
