# Consumer Commission Complaint Preparation & Case-Readiness Tool

## Statutory Framework
The **Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 35 of 2019)** replaces the erstwhile 1986 Act, introducing enhanced consumer rights, e-filing via e-Jagriti (e-Daakhil), product liability, and revised pecuniary limits.

### Pecuniary Jurisdiction (Notified 2021 Rules)
Jurisdiction is determined strictly on the basis of the **consideration paid** for the goods or services, rather than the total compensation claimed:

1. **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (DCDRC)**:
   - Consideration paid: **Up to ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs)**.
   - Territorial Jurisdiction: Where complainant resides or works, or where opposite party resides/carries on business, or where cause of action arose.
2. **State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (SCDRC)**:
   - Consideration paid: **Exceeding ₹50 Lakhs up to ₹2,00,00,000 (₹2 Crores)**.
3. **National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC)**:
   - Consideration paid: **Exceeding ₹2,00,00,000 (₹2 Crores)**.

---

## E-Filing via e-Jagriti / e-Daakhil Portal
- Consumers can file their complaint online through **[e-Daakhil](https://edaakhil.nic.in/)** without physical registry visits.
- Digital payment of statutory court fee (fee is nil for claims up to ₹5 Lakhs).
- Video-conferencing hearings permitted under CPA 2019 provisions.

---

## Statutory Timelines & Limitations
- **Limitation Period (Section 69)**: Complaint must be filed within **2 years** from the date on which the cause of action arose. Delay may be condoned if sufficient cause is demonstrated.
- **Admission Hearing (Section 36)**: Commission must decide on admissibility within **21 days** of filing.
- **Resolution Target**: Endeavour to resolve within 3 months (or 5 months if laboratory testing of goods is ordered).

---

## Essential Evidentiary Dossier
- Tax invoice / retail bill / booking confirmation showing consideration paid.
- Proof of delivery / installation / transaction statement.
- Written communication with opposite party (emails, customer support tickets, pre-litigation notice).
- Photographs / expert reports evidencing defect or deficiency.
- Chronological timeline affidavit.

---

## Self-Representation Right & Disclaimer
> **Section 35 & Rule 12**: A consumer is entitled to represent their own case in person (*party-in-person*) without any mandatory requirement to engage an advocate. This tool prepares a formatted memorandum and case-readiness summary for informational empowerment.
