# Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) Issuance & Transfer Guide (India)

## Statutory Context
- Governed by the **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988** (amended 2019) and the **Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989**.
- Regulated by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India.
- Unified digital platform: **Vahan Citizen Services** (`vahan.parivahan.gov.in`).

## Statutory Forms & Operational Pathways
1. **New Vehicle Registration (Form 20)**:
   - Dealer-assisted online registration or direct RTO submission.
   - Requires Sale Certificate (Form 21), Roadworthiness Certificate (Form 22), Insurance Certificate, Proof of Address, and road tax payment receipt.
   - High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) fitted before delivery.
2. **Transfer of Ownership (Normal Sale - Form 29 & Form 30)**:
   - **Form 29**: Notice of transfer of ownership by the seller.
   - **Form 30**: Report of transfer and application by the purchaser.
   - Within same RTO jurisdiction: Direct ownership endorsement.
   - Between different RTOs in the same state: Transfer with clearance certificate (CC) / internal NOC.
   - Inter-State Transfer: Requires **Form 28 (No Objection Certificate - NOC)** from original registering RTO, road tax payment at destination state, and subsequent refund claim from source state.
3. **Transfer of Ownership on Death of Owner (Form 31)**:
   - Succession / legal heir transfer within 30 days of demise informing RTO, and within 3 months for title transfer.
   - Requires Death Certificate, Legal Heirship / Succession Certificate, NOC from other legal heirs.
4. **Hypothecation Endorsement & Cancellation (HP - Form 34 & Form 35)**:
   - **Form 34**: Addition/Endorsement of hypothecation when vehicle is financed by a bank/NBFC.
   - **Form 35**: Termination / Cancellation of hypothecation upon loan closure, accompanied by Bank NOC and Form 35 duplicate.
5. **Duplicate RC (Form 26)**:
   - Applied when original RC is lost, destroyed, torn, or mutilated.
   - Requires Police Loss Report / e-FIR acknowledgement, valid Insurance, and PUCC.
6. **Change of Address in RC (Form 33)**:
   - Within 30 days of moving residence under Section 49 of MVA.
   - Requires new address proof and valid chassis imprint / inspection.
