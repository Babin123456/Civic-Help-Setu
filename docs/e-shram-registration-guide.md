# e-Shram Registration & Profile Update Guide with Benefit Caveats (India)

## Statutory & Administrative Context
- Administered by the **Ministry of Labour & Employment (MoLE)**, Government of India.
- Launched under the **Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, 2008** to create a National Database of Unorganised Workers (NDUW).
- Official Portal: `eshram.gov.in` (Unified portal for unorganised worker registration).

## What the e-Shram UAN Card Is vs. What It Is NOT
1. **What It Is**:
   - A 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) card assigning a portable national identification number to unorganised workers.
   - Central repository capturing worker occupation, skills, educational level, family details, and bank account credentials.
   - Integrates unorganised workers into government welfare databases for disaster relief, accidental death insurance (PMSBY linkage when announced), and targeted transfer programs.
2. **Critical Caveats & Misconceptions**:
   - **NOT an Automatic Money / Cash Scheme**: Registration does NOT grant automatic monthly pensions, regular cash transfers, or universal basic income.
   - **NOT Free Health Insurance**: Does not replace Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY).
   - **Requires Separate Scheme Eligibility**: Access to specific welfare benefits (e.g., PM-SYM pension, PMMVY, housing) requires meeting the independent statutory eligibility rules of those distinct schemes.
   - **Exclusions**: Any worker who is an income tax payee or a member of EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organization) / ESIC (Employees' State Insurance Corporation) is strictly ineligible.

## Registration & Profile Updates
- **Self-Registration**: 100% free via Aadhaar OTP on `eshram.gov.in`.
- **Assisted Route**: Common Service Centres (CSC) / State Seva Kendras (free of charge to the worker; CSCs are reimbursed by the Ministry).
- **Profile Updates**: Address, mobile number, occupation code (NCO - National Classification of Occupations), and bank account details can be updated anytime using Aadhaar-linked OTP.
