# Domestic Worker Rights and Welfare Scheme Information Hub

## Overview
Domestic workers and residential employers often struggle to locate verified statutory labour protections, state welfare board registration procedures, grievance redressal helplines, and social security benefit funds.

## Core Capabilities
1. **State & Worker Category Mapping (`domesticWorkerWelfareData.js`)**:
   - Covers central e-Shram alongside state-specific boards (e.g. Maharashtra Domestic Workers Welfare Board, Karnataka Unorganised Workers Social Security Board).
   - Clear breakdown of registration routes, fees, and official portals.
2. **Statutory Protections vs. Scheme Benefits (`domesticWorkerWelfareService.js`)**:
   - Explicitly separates legally mandated rights (minimum wages, POSH Act Local Complaints Committees, weekly off) from scheme-specific welfare aids (maternity assistance, accidental insurance, education grants).
3. **No-PII Privacy Protection**:
   - Does not solicit or store employer or domestic worker identity papers.
4. **Community Correction Workflow**:
   - Provides an editorial dispute submission mechanism to report superseded rules or updated fee schedules.
