# UP Disaster Relief Camp Resource and Accessibility Status Network

## Overview
During annual monsoon flooding (Ganga, Yamuna, Saryu/Ghaghra, Rapti, Gandak river basins) and seasonal climatic emergencies across Uttar Pradesh, thousands of families are temporarily displaced into disaster relief shelters.

Administered by the **Office of the Relief Commissioner, Uttar Pradesh (`rahat.up.gov.in`)** and the **UP State Disaster Management Authority (UPSDMA)**, effective camp management requires real-time transparency of camp intake, essential amenities, and vulnerable group accessibility.

---

## Core Humanitarian Amenities & Minimum Sphere Standards

1. **Potable Drinking Water**:
   - Continuous supply of halogenated/chlorinated drinking water tankers or functional deep tube-wells.
2. **Gender-Separated Sanitation**:
   - Clear physical separation between female and male toilets/bathing cubicles with interior locks.
3. **Medical Counter & ORS / Anti-Venom**:
   - Availability of daily PHC medical visits, Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) packets, water-purification chlorine tablets, and snakebite anti-venom at nearby CHCs.
4. **Infant & Child Care**:
   - Supply of fresh milk and infant nutritional porridge (Dalia/Khichdi).
5. **Mobile Device Charging & Illumination**:
   - Solar lanterns or diesel generator (DG) charging hubs enabling families to contact separated relatives.

---

## Vulnerable Group & Disability Accessibility
Relief camps frequently fail to accommodate disabled persons (Divyangjan) and frail senior citizens:
- **Wheelchair Accessibility**: Temporary wooden or concrete ramps at entry thresholds.
- **Ground-Floor Priority**: Reserving cots/bedding on ground level to eliminate stairway climbing.
- **Illuminated Night Pathways**: Non-slippery, lit paths connecting living areas to toilets.
- **Accessible Toilets**: Provision of raised commode chairs.

---

## Situation Reporting (SitRep) & Escalation
- Automated situation reports (SitReps) summarize live capacity vs occupancy and highlight critical supply deficits.
- **District Emergency Operations Centre (DEOC)**: Operates 24x7 under ADM (Finance & Revenue) and District Magistrate.
- **UP State Emergency Helpline**: Toll-Free **1070** (State Disaster Control Room) or **112**.

---

## Humanitarian Privacy Framework
- Under UP disaster management protocols, the dignity of displaced citizens is paramount.
- The service tracks purely physical camp facilities, commodity inventories, and capacity metrics.
- **Strict Prohibition**: No names, biometric IDs, or photographs of displaced individuals/families are captured or distributed.


## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Highlights for Camps
1. **Administrative Designation**: Each relief camp is assigned an Executive Magistrate / Naib Tehsildar as Camp In-Charge.
2. **Health Surveillance**: A mobile medical team conducts bi-daily screenings for acute diarrheal disease (ADD), malaria, dengue, and snake envenomation.
3. **Potable Water Quality Assurance**: Mandatory deployment of mobile water testing kits (chloroscope) to ensure residual chlorine levels between 0.2 to 0.5 ppm.
4. **Child & Infant Nutrition**: Priority allocation of packaged cow milk, biscuits, and boiled baby water.
5. **Security Protocols**: Round-the-clock female police deployment at dormitory wings and sanitary zones.


## Community Verification & Volunteer Checklist
- **Shelter Intake Verification**: Ensure displaced persons are guided to open shelters with available bed space.
- **Supply Escalation**: Flag missing infant formula, dry firewood, or drinking water directly to the District Emergency Operations Centre (DEOC).
- **Vulnerable Family Support**: Ensure women, lactating mothers, children, and disabled individuals receive priority cot assignments and dry change of clothes.
- **Zero Exploitation Norms**: Immediate reporting of unauthorized individuals soliciting fees or identity cards for free disaster relief food or shelter.
