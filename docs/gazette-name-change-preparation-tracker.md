# Gazette Name-Change Preparation and Publication Tracker

This document details the legal workflow, applicant categories, milestone tracking, and post-publication updates for the Gazette Name-Change Preparation Workspace in Civic Help Setu.

## Problem Statement
Changing one's legal name in India is a multi-step statutory procedure spanning several weeks. Citizens frequently misunderstand the legal hierarchy:
1. **Affidavit vs. Gazette**: Executing a local stamp-paper affidavit or publishing a newspaper notice alone is legally insufficient for official document changes (such as Central Government passports or educational boards).
2. **Post-Gazette Updates Are NOT Automatic**: Publication in Part IV of The Gazette of India legally notifies the public of the name change, but does **not** push electronic updates to Aadhaar, Income Tax (PAN), Passport Seva, or commercial banks. Citizens must apply separately to each authority with a certified copy of the gazette issue.
3. **Applicant Variations**: Minors require joint parental declarations and birth certificates; post-marriage updates have distinct documentation compared to gender-affirmation name changes.

## Four-Phase Procedure Map

```
  Phase 1: Notarized Affidavit
  ├── ₹10 - ₹100 Non-Judicial Stamp Paper
  ├── Executed before Notary Public / Executive Magistrate
  └── Minor applicants: Joint declaration by both parents / guardians
           │
           ▼
  Phase 2: Dual Newspaper Publication
  ├── 1 Local Regional Language Daily (circulating in applicant's district)
  ├── 1 Leading English National Daily
  └── Preserve full unclipped original newspaper pages with dates
           │
           ▼
  Phase 3: Central Gazette Submission (Department of Publication)
  ├── Statutory Fee: ₹1,100 (Adult) / ₹1,700 (Minor) via BharatKosh
  ├── Proforma hard copy + digital soft copy in CD
  ├── 2 original newspaper sheets + original attested affidavit
  └── Published in Part IV of Weekly Gazette of India (eGazette.gov.in)
           │
           ▼
  Phase 4: Post-Gazette Individual Record Updates (SEPARATE STEPS)
  ├── Aadhaar Update: Demographic update at Aadhaar Seva Kendra
  ├── PAN Update: Form 49A correction via Protean/UTIITSL
  ├── Passport: Reissue under "Change of Name" on Passport Seva
  └── Banks & Educational Institutions: KYC record update with gazette PDF
```

## Milestone Tracker (Zero-PII Storage)
The tracker records anonymous milestone status codes (`PENDING`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `VERIFIED`) across the 8 statutory touchpoints. Neither the old name, new name, nor any government ID number is stored or transmitted in application state.

### Exportable Readiness Summary
Citizens can generate a clean markdown summary of completed phases and pending requirements to take to local notary offices or post to the Department of Publication without exposing sensitive personal identification.
