# Public Cremation and Burial Service Cost and Required-Document Guide

## Context & Objectives
During bereavement, grieving families face immense emotional distress while trying to identify operating hours, published municipal rates, hearse accessibility, and mandatory documentation required by municipal crematoria and burial grounds. In some instances, families are exploited by unauthorized touts demanding excessive fees.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Verified Municipal Facilities Directory (`cremationBurialFacilitiesData.js`)**:
   - Covers Bengaluru (BBMP electric crematoria, Harishchandra Ghat, nominal ₹250 fee, free for BPL).
   - Covers Delhi (MCD Nigambodh Ghat, CNG furnace rates, 24x7 emergency slots).
2. **Mandatory Documentation Requirements (`cremationBurialGuideService.js`)**:
   - Form 4/4A Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCD).
   - Police Panchnama / NOC (mandated exclusively in unnatural deaths).
   - Identity proofs of deceased and next-of-kin.
3. **Post-Funeral Death Registration Pathway**:
   - Clear guidance on converting crematorium acknowledgement receipts into official Death Certificates within the 21-day statutory window under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969.
4. **Compassionate & Respectful Privacy Architecture**:
   - Low-bandwidth printable design. Zero collection of personal bereavement information.
