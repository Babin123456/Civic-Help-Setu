# UP Birth-and-Death Registration Correction & Delayed-Entry Navigator

## Overview
Under the **Registration of Births and Deaths (RBD) Act, 1969** and the **Uttar Pradesh Registration of Births and Deaths Rules, 2002**, civil registration of vital events is compulsory across Uttar Pradesh.

When citizens miss the statutory reporting period or encounter typographical errors on issued certificates, navigating multiple revenue and municipal offices becomes confusing.

This service clarifies statutory timelines, evidentiary thresholds, late fees, and judicial orders required under UP law.

---

## Timelines & Statutory Pathways (Section 13)

| Period Elapsed | Statutory Classification | Competent Authority | Prescribed Fee | Mandatory Documents |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 – 21 Days** | Normal / On-Time | Local Registrar (GVA / Nagar Nigam / Hospital) | **Free (₹0)** | Form 1/2, Hospital slip or Gram Pradhan verification. |
| **21 – 30 Days** | Delayed (Tier 1) | Local Registrar with late fee | ₹2.00 | Self-declaration explaining delay. |
| **30 – 365 Days** | Delayed (Tier 2) | Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Tehsildar | ₹5.00 | SDM order, Notary affidavit, Non-Availability Certificate (NAC Form 10). |
| **> 1 Year** | Delayed (Tier 3) | First Class Magistrate / SDM Court Order | ₹10.00 | Judicial Inquiry Order under Sec 13(3), Form 10 NAC, Police/Lekhpal report, school/burial proof. |

---

## Non-Availability Certificate (NAC / Form 10)
For any event delayed past 30 days, the applicant must first apply for an inspection of the register at the local Tehsil or Municipal office:
- If no prior registration exists, the Registrar issues **Form 10 (Non-Availability Certificate)**.
- Form 10 serves as a condition precedent before the SDM can accept an application for delayed entry.

---

## Correction of Errors in Issued Certificates (Section 15 & Rule 11)

### 1. Clerical or Typographical Errors
- **Rule**: If a spelling error occurred during data entry (e.g. name of child or parents).
- **Procedure**: Approved by the **Local Registrar**. The applicant must produce two credible residents who knew the family when the birth/death occurred, along with supporting documents (e.g. school certificate or hospital discharge card).
- **Legal Note**: The Registrar does not erase the original text; an official marginal entry correcting the error is recorded, signed, and dated.

### 2. Substantial Name Changes or Adoption
- Requires a court adoption decree, judicial deed, or State Gazette publication.

## Step-by-Step Magistrate Application Checklist
1. **Form 10 (Non-Availability Certificate)**: Secure this from the local Registrar prior to approaching the SDM court.
2. **Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) Court Notice**: File an application under Section 13(3) of RBD Act along with the generated sworn affidavit.
3. **Field Inquiry Order**: SDM marks the file to the local Lekhpal / Revenue Inspector or police station for spot verification.
4. **Final Judicial Order**: Upon receiving favorable inquiry reports, SDM issues a formal order instructing the Registrar to record the entry in the register.
5. **Entry and Certificate Delivery**: Registrar collects the ₹10 statutory fee and issues the certified extract.
