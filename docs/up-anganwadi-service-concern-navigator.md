# UP Anganwadi Service Access and Facility Concern Navigator

## Overview
Under the **Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)** programme administered by the Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh (`balvikasup.gov.in`), over 1.8 lakh Anganwadi Centres (AWCs) provide early childhood care and nutritional security.

Despite substantial public investment and the digital rollout of **Poshan Tracker**, rural and peri-urban centres frequently suffer from chronic supply disruptions of supplementary nutrition (Take Home Ration - THR), locked or unhygienic toilets, and non-functional weighing scales.

This module empowers mothers, adolescent girls, and community groups to navigate their statutory entitlements and log constructive, privacy-preserving facility concerns.

---

## 6 Core ICDS Services & Entitlements

1. **Supplementary Nutrition (Poshan Aahar / THR)**:
   - Prescribed for children 6 months to 6 years, pregnant women, and nursing mothers.
   - Mandated for at least **25 days per month (300 days a year)**.
2. **Growth Monitoring & Nutritional Tracking**:
   - Monthly weight-for-age and height-for-age measurement logged on the government Poshan Tracker portal to detect Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) and Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM).
3. **Early Childhood Care & Education (ECCE / Balvatika)**:
   - Daily morning play-way preschool activities for children aged 3 to 6 years.
4. **Health Check-up & Referral Services**:
   - Monthly health checkups and referrals to Community Health Centres (CHC) for sick or malnourished infants.
5. **Immunization Convergence (VHSND / Aarogya Diwas)**:
   - Held on fixed Wednesdays or Saturdays in coordination with the village Auxiliary Nurse Midwife (ANM) and ASHA worker.
6. **Nutrition & Health Education (NHE)**:
   - Counseling for mothers on infant feeding practices, breastfeeding, and dietary diversity.

## Escalation Matrix & Supervision Hierarchy

| Tier | Administrative Authority | Core Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Sector Level** | Mukhya Sevika (Supervisor) | Inspects 20–25 AWCs monthly; audits stock registers and Poshan Tracker attendance. |
| **Block Level** | Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) | Manages block-level THR buffer godown and administrative staff discipline. |
| **District Level** | District Programme Officer (DPO) | Directs district-wide ICDS policy and initiates disciplinary action for supply diversion. |

## Strict Child & Family Privacy Safeguards
- **Zero Minor PII**: No names of infants, personal medical diagnostics, Aadhaar numbers, or photographs of children are captured or published.
- **Physical Quality Focus**: The system focuses strictly on infrastructure, commodity supply continuity, and civil amenities.
- **Community Consensus**: Public flags require at least 3 independent observations from the same locality before formal escalation.


## Community Monitoring & Mother-Guardian Checklist
1. **Poshan Tracker Transparency**: Ensure monthly height and weight entries are recorded in the worker's application and explained in Hindi to the mother.
2. **Weekly Recipe Board**: AWC must display the weekly menu indicating whether Hot Cooked Meals or fortified THR grains were delivered on schedule.
3. **Safe Storage**: Grains and milk powder packets must be stored elevated on wooden pallets away from damp floors and rodents.
4. **First Aid & Health Kiosk**: Availability of basic first-aid supplies (cotton, antiseptic liquid, paracetamol syrup, iron-folic acid tablets).
5. **Constructive Community Follow-Up**: Always engage with the local Gram Panchayat Ward Member or Mahila Arogya Samiti before filing formal administrative complaints.


## Procedural Workflow with Civic Help Setu
- Community observations undergo an automated deduplication filter ensuring spam from single devices does not skew center quality metrics.
- Exported memorandums are ready to be attached to official IGRS/Jan Sunwai petitions or physically handed over during Tehsil Diwas meetings.
- Protects whistleblowers and local mothers from social intimidation by providing anonymous submission workflows.
