# Small Business Trade Licence Renewal and Compliance Checklist

## Context & Objectives
Small shopkeepers, food stall operators, and micro-enterprises frequently confuse municipal trade licences with Shops & Establishments registrations, FSSAI food certificates, and GST numbers. Many face sudden municipal inspections, penalty compounding, or trade sealing for delayed renewals.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Jurisdiction-Specific Renewal Frameworks (`tradeLicenceRegistryData.js`)**:
   - Covers BBMP Bengaluru (February 28 deadline, health wing fee slabs, property tax linkage).
   - Covers MCD Delhi (March 31 deadline, factory/general trade licensing department).
2. **Distinct Regulatory Regime Demarcation (`tradeLicenceChecklistService.js`)**:
   - Explicit matrix distinguishing Municipal Trade Licences (nuisance/fire safety) from State Shops & Establishments (labour hours/welfare), FSSAI (food purity), and GST (taxation).
3. **Renewal Checklist & Payment Receipt Verification**:
   - Steps to inspect digital challans, verify QR codes, and retain printed licences conspicuously on shop premises.
4. **Anti-Overclaim Protection**:
   - Explicit disclaimers clarifying that obtaining a trade licence does not exempt business owners from other required statutory permits.
