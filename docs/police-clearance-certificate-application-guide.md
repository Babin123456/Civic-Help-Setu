# Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) Application Guide (India)

## Statutory Context & Issuing Authorities
In India, there are two fundamentally distinct pathways for obtaining a Police Clearance Certificate (PCC), depending entirely on the destination/receiving authority:

1. **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) PCC (Ministry of External Affairs - MEA)**:
   - **Governed by**: The Passports Act, 1967.
   - **Issuing Authority**: Regional Passport Office (RPO) / Consular, Passport & Visa (CPV) Division, MEA.
   - **Primary Use**: Foreign immigration, long-term employment visas, permanent residency (PR), student visas, or citizenship abroad.
   - **Process**: Online application on `passportindia.gov.in`, in-person appointment at PSK/POPSK for biometric capture and document verification, followed by local jurisdictional police verification report (PVR).
   - **Statutory Fee**: ₹500 standard government fee.
   - **Validity**: Typically valid for 6 months from issuance date (as required by foreign embassies).
2. **Local / State Police Character / Clearance Certificate**:
   - **Governed by**: State Police Acts and citizen service portals (e.g., Delhi Police PCC, Maharashtra Police Verification, UP Police CCTNS).
   - **Primary Use**: Domestic private / corporate employment, armed security guards, school bus drivers, tenancy verification, government tenders.
   - **Issuing Authority**: Commissioner of Police / Superintendent of Police (SP) of the respective district.
   - **Process**: Online submission via state CCTNS portal, background criminal check across CCTNS database, and delivery via digital PDF.

## Critical Caveat
Never apply for a state police certificate when a foreign embassy (e.g., US Embassy, UK Home Office, IRCC Canada, Australian Immigration) specifically mandates an **MEA Passport Seva PCC**. Foreign embassies reject state police slips.
