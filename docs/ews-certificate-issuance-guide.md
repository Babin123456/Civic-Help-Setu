# Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Certificate Issuance Guide — Research Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#506 — Add an EWS Certificate issuance guide for central and state use](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/506)  
**Scope:** Constitutional 103rd Amendment Act, 2019, Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT OM No. 36039/1/2019-Estt.(Res)), Ministry of Social Justice guidelines, and state-level EWS variations.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** An EWS (Income and Asset) Certificate provides 10% reservation in civil posts and educational admissions for citizens belonging to Economically Weaker Sections who are **not covered** under the scheme of reservation for SCs, STs, and OBCs.
- **What It Does NOT Do:**
  - It does not apply to SC, ST, or OBC candidates (even if economically underprivileged).
  - It does not guarantee admission or job selection without meeting merit cut-offs.
  - Validity is strictly linked to a specific Financial Year and preceding Assessment Year.

---

## 2. Central EWS Income and Asset Criteria (DoPT Standard)

Persons whose family meets the following conditions are eligible for Central EWS benefits:
1. **Gross Annual Family Income:** Below **₹8 Lakhs** from all sources (salary, agriculture, business, profession) for the financial year prior to the year of application.
2. **Exclusion Asset Criteria:** Families holding any of the following assets are excluded from EWS regardless of gross income:
   - 5 acres of agricultural land and above;
   - Residential flat of 1000 sq. ft. and above;
   - Residential plot of 100 sq. yards and above in notified municipalities;
   - Residential plot of 200 sq. yards and above in areas other than the notified municipalities.

---

## 3. Definition of "Family" for EWS

Under DoPT guidelines:
- Candidate applying for reservation.
- Parents of the candidate.
- Siblings below the age of 18 years.
- Spouse of the candidate.
- Children of the candidate below the age of 18 years.
*(Note: Married adult siblings or grandparents are excluded from the family asset pool under central guidelines).*

---

## 4. Assessment Year vs. Validity Year

- **Financial Year of Validity:** The financial year in which the certificate is issued and utilized (e.g. FY 2026-27).
- **Assessment Year / Income Period:** The preceding financial year whose financial records and tax returns are evaluated (e.g. FY 2025-26).
- An EWS certificate printed without mentioning both the **Valid Year** and the **Assessment Year** is routinely rejected in central document verification (UPSC, NTA, SSC).

---

## 5. Competent Authorities to Issue EWS Certificate

1. District Magistrate / Additional District Magistrate / Collector / Deputy Commissioner.
2. Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Taluka Magistrate / Executive Magistrate.
3. Chief Presidency Magistrate / Additional Chief Presidency Magistrate.
4. Revenue Officer not below the rank of Tehsildar.
5. Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) of the area where candidate and/or family normally resides.
