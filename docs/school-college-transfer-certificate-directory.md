# Public School & College Transfer-Certificate Process Directory

## Legal & Statutory Foundations
The right to transfer between educational institutions and acquire proof of educational attainment is safeguarded under national educational statutes and regulatory bye-laws.

---

## 1. School Transfer Certificate (TC) / School Leaving Certificate (SLC)
- **Issuing Body**: Headmaster / Principal of the recognized elementary or secondary school.
- **RTE Act 2009 Section 5 Protection**:
  - *Section 5(2)*: A child has the fundamental statutory right to seek transfer to any other school.
  - *Section 5(3)*: The Head-teacher is legally mandated to **immediately issue** the transfer certificate.
  - **Withholding Safeguard**: A delay or dispute concerning school fees **shall not be a ground for delaying or denying admission** in another school.
  - Failure to issue TC attracts disciplinary proceedings against the school administration.

---

## 2. Board Migration Certificate
- **Issuing Body**: Educational Board of Examination (e.g., Central Board of Secondary Education - CBSE, CISCE, State Secondary / Higher Secondary Councils).
- **Purpose**: Required when migrating across educational boards (e.g., CBSE to State Pre-University or University).
- **Digital Access**: CBSE and many state boards issue digitally verified migration certificates on **DigiLocker** with equal legal validity under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
- **Physical Issue**: Available through online portals (e.g., CBSE Pariksha Sangam) with tracking via speed post.

---

## 3. College Leaving Certificate (CLC) & University Migration
- **Issuing Body**: College Registrar / University Examination Controller.
- **Clearance Process**: Institutional clearance required (Library no-dues, laboratory breakage clearance, hostel dues).
- **UGC Guidelines**: Higher education institutions are prohibited from retaining original secondary school certificates or denying CLC arbitrarily.

---

## Duplicate & Correction Procedures

### Duplicate Issuance (Loss or Damage)
1. File an online Police Lost Report / Non-Cognizable Report (NCR).
2. Notarized affidavit on stamp paper stating date of loss and undertaking that original has not been misused.
3. Submission to issuing authority for re-issue marked **"DUPLICATE"**.

### Correction of Name / Date of Birth
1. Submission of municipal birth certificate or State Gazette notification.
2. Verification against early admission registers (SR register).
3. Board bye-law compliance for publication in local newspapers if required.
