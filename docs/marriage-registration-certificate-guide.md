# Marriage Registration Certificate Guide — State-Specific Procedures Research Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#503 — Add a Marriage Registration Certificate guide for state-specific procedures](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/503)  
**Scope:** Statutory marriage registration across India under the Special Marriage Act, 1954, the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, and state compulsory marriage registration acts/rules.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** A Marriage Registration Certificate is conclusive legal proof of the solemnisation and registration of a marriage under Indian law. It is required for passport spousal endorsement, joint bank accounts, visa applications, and inheritance claims.
- **Solemnisation vs. Registration:**
  - *Solemnisation* refers to the performance of ceremonies, rites, or rituals according to religious personal laws or a civil declaration before a Marriage Officer.
  - *Registration* is the official recording of the marriage by a government Marriage Registrar.
  - While registration provides conclusive legal documentation, failure to register under religious personal laws does not necessarily invalidate a properly solemnised customary marriage, though registration is legally compulsory in many states.

---

## 2. Applicable Legal Acts in India

1. **Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 (Section 8):**
   - Applies where both parties are Hindu, Buddhist, Jaina, or Sikh.
   - Post-marriage registration of an already solemnised marriage.
   - Jurisdiction: Office of Sub-Registrar / Marriage Registrar where the marriage was solemnised OR where either spouse has resided for at least 6 months (or 30 days depending on state rules).
2. **Special Marriage Act, 1954:**
   - Civil marriage for any Indian citizens regardless of religion.
   - Requires a 30-day public notice period at the Marriage Officer's office.
   - If no valid legal objections are filed within 30 days, solemnisation/registration occurs in the presence of 3 witnesses.
3. **State Compulsory Registration Acts:**
   - E.g., Tamil Nadu Registration of Marriages Act, 2009; Delhi Compulsory Marriage Registration Order; Maharashtra Regulation of Marriage Bureaus and Registration of Marriages Act.

---

## 3. Statutory Witnesses & Proof Requirements

- **Witness Requirements:**
  - Special Marriage Act: 3 adult witnesses must be physically present with identity and address proofs.
  - Hindu Marriage Act: 2 to 3 witnesses (varies by state rules; e.g. parents, relatives, or known adults).
- **Essential Documents:**
  - Age proof for both parties (Birth certificate, 10th marksheet, or Passport) confirming Groom ≥ 21 years and Bride ≥ 18/21 years.
  - Address proof of bride and groom in the registration jurisdiction.
  - Wedding invitation card or certificate of marriage from priest/religious place (for already solemnised marriages).
  - Joint wedding photograph and passport-size photographs of bride, groom, and witnesses.
  - Affidavits of marital status (unmarried, divorced with decree, or widowed with death certificate of former spouse).
