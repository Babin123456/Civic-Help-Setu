# Cyber-Fraud First-Response and Evidence Preservation Guide

## Context & Objectives
When citizens fall victim to digital payment fraud, screen-sharing scams (AnyDesk/TeamViewer), or digital arrest impersonation, the first 1-2 hours ("Golden Hours") are critical to freezing stolen funds. However, victims frequently panic, delete evidence, or search fraudulent customer care numbers on Google.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Scenario-Based Guidance (`cyberFraudEmergencyData.js`)**:
   - UPI/QR Code Fraud: 12-digit UTR preservation and bank lien protocols.
   - Remote Access Takeover: Immediate offline/airplane mode isolation and de-linking.
   - Digital Arrest Impersonation: Clear notification that real law enforcement agencies never conduct digital arrests via video call.
2. **Local Evidence Preservation & Dossier (`cyberFraudResponseGuideService.js`)**:
   - Checklists for saving screenshots, timestamps, SMS alerts, and call records without crop or alteration.
   - Generates a printable incident timeline for 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in submission.
3. **Strict Credential Safeguard**:
   - Zero collection of OTPs, PINs, passwords, or full card details.
4. **Channel Separation**:
   - Distinguishes immediate bank freezing actions from police FIR / cybercrime investigation routes.
