## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** An Indian Passport is an official national travel document issued by the President of India through the Ministry of External Affairs under the Passports Act, 1967.
- **What It Proves:**
  - Conclusive proof of Indian nationality and identity for international travel.
  - Prima facie proof of full legal name, date of birth, place of birth, and permanent address.
- **What It Does NOT Do:**
  - It does NOT guarantee foreign entry; visas or visa-free permits remain subject to foreign sovereign immigration laws.
  - Possession of dual citizenship/foreign passports is prohibited under Article 9 of the Constitution of India; foreign naturalization mandates immediate surrender of Indian passport.

## 2. Service Categories: Fresh vs. Re-issue & Tatkaal

1. **Fresh Passport:** Applied when the individual has never held an Indian passport before.
2. **Re-issue of Passport:** Applied for when:
   - Validity has expired or is expiring within 1 year;
   - Pages in existing passport booklet are exhausted;
   - Change in personal particulars (marital status, surname, address, spouse addition);
   - Damaged, mutilated, or lost passport.
3. **Normal vs. Tatkaal Scheme:**
   - **Normal Scheme:** Statutory fee of ₹1,500 (36 pages) / ₹2,000 (60 pages). Processed via standard police verification.
   - **Tatkaal Scheme:** Statutory fee of ₹3,500 (36 pages) / ₹4,000 (60 pages). Dispatched within 1–3 business days without waiting for pre-police verification (post-issuance verification applies).

---

## 3. The 3 Tiers of Police Verification (PVR)

1. **Pre-Police Verification:** Police visit applicant's residence and submit physical character report before the Regional Passport Office (RPO) can grant the passport (standard for fresh adult passports).
2. **Post-Police Verification:** Passport is granted immediately and dispatched; police verification occurs after receipt (applied for Tatkaal, government servants, or specific renewal categories).
3. **No Police Verification:** Applied when re-issuing a passport within validity with no change in address and previous clear police records, or for minor children with valid parental passports.

## 4. PSK / POPSK Appointment Counters (A, B, C)

At the Passport Seva Kendra visit:
- **Counter A (TCS / Service Partner):** Biometric capture (fingerprints, digital facial photo) and document upload.
- **Counter B (Government Verification Officer):** Physical inspection of original certificates (birth proof, 10th marksheet for Non-ECR status, address proofs).
- **Counter C (Granting Officer / RPO Executive):** Final decision on passport approval, application status grant, or referral for police inquiry.
