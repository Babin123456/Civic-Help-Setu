# Permanent Driving Licence (DL) Issuance, Test & Renewal Guide (India)

## Statutory Context
- Governed by the **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988** (amended 2019) and the **Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), 1989**.
- Regulated by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India.
- Managed digitally through the national centralized portal **Sarathi Parivahan** (`sarathi.parivahan.gov.in`).

## Key Regulatory Milestones & Rules
1. **From Learner's Licence (LL) to Permanent DL**:
   - Applicant must hold an active Learner's Licence for **at least 30 days** before booking a permanent driving test slot.
   - Permanent DL must be applied for within **180 days (6 months)** of LL issuance before the LL expires. If LL expires, a new LL must be applied for.
2. **Vehicle Categories**:
   - **MCWOG / MC50CC**: Motorcycle without gear (moped up to 50cc) - Age 16+ (with parental consent).
   - **MCWG**: Motorcycle with gear - Age 18+.
   - **LMV**: Light Motor Vehicle (cars, jeeps, delivery vans under 7,500 kg unladen) - Age 18+.
   - **Transport / Commercial**: Heavy/Medium Goods or Passenger Vehicle - Age 20+; requires 1 year prior holding of LMV licence and medical Form 1A.
3. **Automated Driving Test Track (ADTT)**:
   - Modern RTO testing utilizes sensor-embedded automated tracks without human examiner bias.
   - Standard 4-wheeler maneuvers: Up-gradient stop and restart without rollback (>30cm), Parallel parking, Reverse 'S' bend / 8-formation, Emergency braking.
   - Standard 2-wheeler maneuvers: Serpentine ('8') track without foot touching ground, ramp balancing.
4. **Renewal Regulations (MVA 2019 Amendment)**:
   - Application for renewal can be initiated **1 year before expiry** or up to **1 year after expiry**.
   - If renewed within 1 year after expiry, no practical re-test is required; validity resumes from original expiry or date of application per state rules.
   - If renewal is sought **more than 1 year after expiry**, applicant must retake and pass the driving skill test under Rule 15 of CMVR.
5. **Medical Fitness (Form 1 / Form 1A)**:
   - Non-transport applicants under 40 years: Self-declaration in Form 1.
   - Non-transport applicants over 40 years or any Transport licence applicant: Form 1A signed by a registered medical practitioner.
6. **Online vs Offline Flow**:
   - Online: Form filling on Sarathi Parivahan, uploading documents (LL, address/age proof), fee payment, slot booking for DL test.
   - In-Person: Attending RTO / ADTT with vehicle in corresponding class (displaying standard 'L' board if driven with licensed escort), biometrics verification, taking practical driving test.
