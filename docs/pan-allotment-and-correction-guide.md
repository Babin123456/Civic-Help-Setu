## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** A Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a ten-digit alphanumeric identifier issued by the Income Tax Department to track financial transactions, tax compliance, and tax deduction at source (TDS).
- **What It Proves:**
  - Certified identity and official tax account identification across India.
- **What It Does NOT Prove:**
  - A PAN Card does **NOT serve as proof of address** or **proof of Indian citizenship** (foreign entities, companies, and non-citizens residing in India are also allotted PANs).
  - Possessing more than one PAN is illegal under Section 272B of the Income Tax Act (punishable with a ₹10,000 fine).

---

## 2. Official Application Channels & Authorised Portals

Only three official government authorized portals process PAN applications in India:
1. **Income Tax e-Filing Portal (`eportal.incometax.gov.in`):**
   - Free Instant e-PAN issuance within 10 minutes using Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP.
   - For adult individual taxpayers only.
2. **Protean eGov Technologies (formerly NSDL):**
   - Physical and digital PAN cards under Form 49A.
   - Published fee: ₹107 (for dispatch within India) / ₹1017 (for dispatch abroad).
3. **UTI Infrastructure Technology and Services Ltd (UTIITSL):**
   - Authorized parallel portal for new allotment, correction, and reprint.

---

## 3. Statutory Forms & Application Pathways

1. **Form 49A:** Application for allotment of new PAN for Indian citizens, HUFs, and domestic companies.
2. **Form 49AA:** Allotment of PAN for foreign citizens, NRIs without Indian Aadhaar, and foreign entities.
3. **PAN Change Request (CSF Form):** For correction of misspelled names, updated photo/signature, or re-issuance of lost/damaged cards.

---

## 4. Key Milestones & Date Tracking

1. **Application / Payment Date:** When the online fee is paid and the 15-digit Acknowledgement Number is generated.
2. **Scrutiny & Verification Date:** Processing of uploaded/physical documents by Protean or UTIITSL.
3. **Allotment Date:** Day the Income Tax Department allocates the 10-digit PAN alphanumeric string. Instant e-PAN can be downloaded immediately.
4. **Dispatch Date:** Date the physical laminated card is handed over to India Post (Speed Post tracking number issued).
5. **Delivery Date:** Final physical delivery to applicant's postal address.
