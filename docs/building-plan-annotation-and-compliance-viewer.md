# Building-Plan Drawing Annotation and Compliance Overlay Viewer

## Overview
Applicants preparing for municipal building plan approvals (e.g. BBMP BPAS, BMC AutoDCR, MCD OBPS) frequently face scrutiny rejections due to omissions in connecting statutory checklist requirements—such as front/rear setbacks, vehicular parking bays, rainwater harvesting pits, and fire egress pathways—with precise physical locations on their architectural drawings.

The `buildingPlanAnnotationService` (`src/services/buildingPlanAnnotationService.js`) introduces a zoom-independent, interactive drawing annotation and compliance overlay viewer. It allows citizens, architects, and scrutiny assistants to visually mark compliance features, receive bounded AI vision suggestions, review proposals with full human confirmation, and export pre-scrutiny review packets accompanied by accessible tabular summaries and statutory disclaimers.

---

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Normalized Drawing Canvas & Coordinate System
- Uses normalized percentage coordinates (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height` from 0.0% to 100.0%) to ensure annotations remain perfectly anchored and aligned across zoom levels, viewport resizes, and multi-page plan sheets.
- Enforces strict boundary validation preventing off-canvas annotations.

---

### 2. Multi-Layer Visual States & Domain Statuses
The visualizer models five distinct annotation states:
1. **`CONFIRMED`**: Verified and explicitly signed off by the applicant or supervising architect (rendered with a bold green indicator).
2. **`USER_MARKED`**: Directly annotated by the user or draughtsman (rendered with a standard blue border).
3. **`AI_SUGGESTED`**: Provisional detection generated by the bounded computer vision assistant (rendered with a dashed amber border).
4. **`UNRESOLVED`**: Mandatory statutory checklist item lacking any corresponding drawing annotation (highlighted with an urgent warning).
5. **`REJECTED`**: AI suggestion reviewed and discarded by the human reviewer, retained internally for auditability.

---

### 3. Linked Statutory Requirements Catalogue
Directly links drawings to authoritative statutory regulations (`STATUTORY_DRAWING_REQUIREMENTS`):
- **`FRONT_SETBACK`**: Clear distance abutting public road (NBC 2016 Part 3 Clause 4.3).
- **`REAR_SETBACK`**: Open yard open to sky for natural light/ventilation (Model Building Bye-Laws Table 4.2).
- **`SIDE_SETBACKS`**: Clear separation margins preventing structural interference.
- **`PARKING_AREA`**: Minimum 2.5m x 5.0m car bays and 3.5m+ circulation driveway (NBC 2016 Part 3 Clause 4.11).
- **`FIRE_EGRESS_AND_STAIRS`**: Enclosed fire-rated staircases with travel distance <= 30m (NBC 2016 Part 4 Clause 4.4).
- **`RAINWATER_HARVESTING`**: Percolation pits and rooftop filter systems.
- **`PLINTH_AND_HEADROOM`**: Plinth min 450mm above road crown; habitable ceiling height min 2.75m.
- **`ROAD_WIDENING_MARGIN`**: Earmarked reservation line for municipal street widening.

---

### 4. Bounded AI Vision Assistant & Guardrails
- **Provisional Status**: AI detections are strictly tagged as `AI_SUGGESTED` and require explicit human confirmation (`reviewAiAnnotation()`) before inclusion in the final review packet.
- **No Automated Certification**: The service displays an explicit statutory disclaimer clarifying that visual proposals do NOT constitute official municipal sanction or structural engineering fitness.
- **Clause-Bounded Explanations**: Explanations cite applicable NBC clauses and municipal bye-laws rather than opaque guesses.

---

### 5. Accessibility & Screen-Reader Alternatives
- `getAccessibleChecklistSummary(sessionId, ownerId)` generates a comprehensive tabular checklist summary describing compliance status, physical coordinates, and statutory references in plain language for screen-reader users and non-visual workflows.

---

### 6. Pre-Scrutiny Export Packet & Privacy Protection
- `exportAnnotationReviewPacket()` compiles an audit dossier complete with readiness verdicts (`READY_FOR_PRE_SCRUTINY` vs `INCOMPLETE_CHECKLIST`), annotation metrics, and visual legend.
- `sanitizeAnnotationPacketForExport()` scrubs sensitive owner identifiers, confidential plan titles, and proprietary user notes prior to external sharing.

## Verification & Testing
Comprehensive Vitest unit tests in `src/services/buildingPlanAnnotationService.test.js` verify:
1. Coordinate normalization and boundary constraint validation (0-100%).
2. Drawing review session initialization and file type verification.
3. User annotation mapping and owner authorization access control.
4. Bounded AI vision proposals and human confirmation/rejection workflows.
5. Undo and delete operations.
6. Accessible tabular summary generation for assistive technologies.
7. Structured pre-scrutiny export packet compilation and privacy sanitization.
