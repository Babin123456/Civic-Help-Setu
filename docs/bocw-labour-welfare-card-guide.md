# Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Welfare Registration Card Guide

## Statutory Context
- Governed by the **Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996** and the **BOCW Welfare Cess Act, 1996**.
- Administered by respective **State BOCW Welfare Boards** (e.g., Delhi BOCW Board, UP BOCW Board, Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board, Karnataka Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board).

## Mandatory Statutory Eligibility
1. **Age Requirement**:
   - Must be between **18 and 60 years** of age.
2. **The 90-Day Work Rule (Section 12)**:
   - The applicant MUST have worked as a building or construction worker for **not less than 90 days** in the preceding 12 months.
   - Proof required: 90-day certificate issued by the registered construction employer/contractor, trade union, registered architect/engineer, or municipal/panchayat certifying officer.
3. **Eligible Occupations**:
   - Masons (Rajmistri), carpenters, painters, plumbers, electricians, bar benders, tile setters, welders, concrete mixers, roller drivers, earth-movers, scaffolding workers, and unskilled manual construction laborers.
4. **Key Differences from General e-Shram**:
   - e-Shram is a generic self-declaration database for all unorganised workers.
   - BOCW registration is **state board-specific**, funded by a statutory 1% building cess collected on construction projects, providing direct statutory cash benefits (scholarships for children, maternity assistance, medical reimbursements, housing loans, and pension on reaching 60).
5. **Subscription & Annual Renewal**:
   - Nominal registration fee (₹20 to ₹50) + monthly/annual welfare subscription (₹10 to ₹100/year).
   - Cardholder must maintain active registration through annual or triennial renewal; non-renewal leads to lapse of beneficiary status.
