# Land Mutation (Dakhil Kharij / Ferfar / Namantaran) Application Guide

## Statutory Context
- Governed by respective State Land Revenue Codes (e.g. **Uttar Pradesh Revenue Code, 2006**, **Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966**, **Bihar Land Reforms Act**, **West Bengal Land Reforms Act, 1955**).
- Under Indian jurisprudence (e.g., Supreme Court rulings in *Bhimabai Mahadeo Kambekar v. Arthur Import and Export Company*, *Sawarni v. Inder Kaur*), **mutation does not confer, create, or extinguish title**. It is an administrative entry for fiscal and land revenue assessment purposes.

## Mutation Pathways
1. **Sale / Transfer Deed Route**:
   - Arises from a registered Sale Deed, Exchange Deed, or Gift Deed executed at the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO).
   - In modern integrated systems (e.g., UP Bhulekh, Banglarbhumi, Aaple Sarkar), auto-mutation (J-Slip / automated SRO-to-Tehsil transmission) triggers upon deed registration.
   - Requires registered deed copy, latest tax receipt, and identification proofs.
2. **Inheritance / Succession (Wirasat / Fauti / Varasai) Route**:
   - Arises upon demise of registered khatadar.
   - Undisputed succession: Natural legal heirs apply with Death Certificate, Surviving Member / Legal Heir Certificate, and Family Tree affidavit.
   - Disputed / Testamentary succession (Will / Wasiyat): Requires probate (in metro jurisdictions under Indian Succession Act) or court declaration before mutation can proceed.
3. **Partition (Batwara) Route**:
   - Formal partition among co-sharers under Section 116 of UP Revenue Code or state equivalent, demarcating distinct khasra numbers and separate revenue khata.
4. **Court Decree / Land Acquisition Route**:
   - Enforcing Civil Court decrees or Section 11/19 Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition (RFCTLARR) orders.

## Statutory Procedural Stages & Timeline Windows
- **Event Date**: Date of sale deed registration or date of demise.
- **Filing / J-Slip Date**: Date of formal application or auto-transmission.
- **Provisional Notice (Form / Istihar)**: Statutory public notice issued by Revenue Inspector / Circle Officer inviting objections within **15 to 35 days**.
- **Objection Hearing Stage**: If objections are filed, case is converted from undisputed (Aavivadit) to contested (Vivadit) and posted before Naib Tehsildar / Tehsildar court.
- **Order & Record Update**: Final mutation order passed, updating Record of Rights (RoR).
