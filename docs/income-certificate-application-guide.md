# Income Certificate Application Guide with Assessment-Period Details — Research Notes

**Scope:** Revenue department guidelines across Indian States/UTs, computation of annual family income, financial assessment year distinction, self-declarations, field verification, and validity periods.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** An Income Certificate is an official document issued by the jurisdictional revenue authority (such as a Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate, or Revenue Officer) certifying the total annual income of an individual and their family from all sources.
- **What It Proves:**
  - Annual family income during the specified Financial Assessment Year.
  - Eligibility for government scholarships (e.g. Post-Matric Scholarship), subsidized welfare schemes, fee waivers, ration cards, and medical assistance funds.
- **What It Does NOT Prove:**
  - It does NOT serve as an Income Tax Return (ITR) or proof of tax assessment under the Income Tax Act.
  - It does NOT verify caste, domicile, or legal heirship.
  - It is strictly valid for the financial year or statutory period (typically 6 months to 1 year) stated on its face by the issuing authority.

---

## 2. Assessment Period vs. Issue Date

- **Financial Year Certified:** Most state revenue rules certify income earned during the **preceding financial year** (1 April to 31 March) or current financial year based on recent salary slips, agricultural yield estimates, and business turnovers.
- **Scheme-Specific Deadlines:** Educational scholarship portals (such as NSP / state welfare portals) frequently require the certificate to reflect the income of the *specific financial year preceding the academic session*, irrespective of when the certificate was downloaded.

---

## 3. Sources of Income Counted

1. **Salaried Income:** Basic pay, DA, allowances (excluding statutory exemptions as notified by state rules).
2. **Business / Trade / Professional Income:** Net earnings from shopkeeping, trading, contracts, or freelance work verified through GST/ITR or local trade tax.
3. **Agricultural Income:** Assessed by the Revenue Inspector / Patwari based on land area, crop pattern, and local harvest circle rates.
4. **Labor & Unorganized Work:** Estimated via local enquiry, ward counselor report, or self-declaration affidavit.

---

## 4. Application Journey & Field Verification

1. **Filing:** Apply via State e-District portal or local CSC / e-Sevai / MeeSeva center.
2. **Scrutiny & Inquiry:** Application is transferred to the Talathi / Village Administrative Officer (VAO) / Patwari.
3. **Spot Verification:** Officer conducts inquiry into the applicant's standard of living, asset ownership, and local business.
4. **Approval & Digital Signature:** Tehsildar reviews the inquiry report and approves the digitally signed certificate with QR verification.
