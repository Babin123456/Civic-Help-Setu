# Ration Card Issuance, Member-Addition, and Correction Guide — Research Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#511 — Add a Ration Card issuance, member-addition, and correction guide](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/511)  
**Scope:** National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013, One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) portability, state Food and Civil Supplies departments, ration card categories (AAY, PHH, NPHH), member addition/deletion workflows, and e-KYC.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** A Ration Card is an official household document issued by the State/UT Food and Civil Supplies Department to purchase subsidized foodgrains under the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS / NFSA).
- **What It Proves:**
  - Household composition, number of family beneficiaries, and category of food entitlement.
  - Recognized widely as secondary proof of address and family relationship.
- **What It Does NOT Prove:**
  - The Supreme Court and Election Commission have clarified that a Ration Card is **not conclusive proof of citizenship**.
  - Possession of a card in one state prohibits having a duplicate card in another state.

---

## 2. Ration Card Categories under NFSA

1. **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY):** Poorest of the poor families; entitled to 35 kg of foodgrains per family per month (Yellow/Pink card depending on state).
2. **Priority Households (PHH):** Households meeting state-defined vulnerability and income criteria; entitled to 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month.
3. **Non-Priority / Above Poverty Line (NPHH / APL):** General households who do not receive central subsidized foodgrain quotas or receive subsidized sugar/kerosene under state subsidies (White card in many states).

---

## 3. Four Core Citizen Paths

1. **New Household Card:** Applied when forming a new independent family unit (requires surrender certificate / deletion certificate from parents' existing ration card).
2. **Member Addition:** Adding a newborn (needs Birth Certificate) or new spouse (needs Marriage Certificate & deletion certificate from previous parental card).
3. **Member Deletion / Surrender:** Deleting a deceased member (needs Death Certificate) or moving spouse/child to another household.
4. **Correction & Address Shifting:** Changing fair price shop (FPS) or correcting misspelled names / date of birth.

---

## 4. One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) & e-KYC

- **National Portability:** Beneficiaries registered under NFSA can lift their subsidized foodgrains from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) across India using biometric authentication (Aadhaar e-POS).
- **Mandatory Aadhaar Seeding:** Every member listed on the card must complete Aadhaar e-KYC verification at an FPS e-POS terminal to prevent cancellation.
