# Property-Tax Rebate, Exemption and Payment Dispute Guide

## Context & Objectives
Property owners across Indian municipal corporations encounter arbitrary assessment hikes, unexplained arrears, and unnotified penal interest. Furthermore, many miss statutory early-payment rebates or struggle to navigate the 30-day window to file formal objections before Assistant Revenue Officers (AROs).

## Key Components
1. **Local-Body Specific Rules (`propertyTaxRulesData.js`)**:
   - Covers BBMP Bengaluru (Unit Area Value, 5% April early-bird rebate, places of worship/ex-servicemen exemptions).
   - Covers MCD Delhi (Unit Area Method, 10% lump sum rebate, 30% senior citizen rebate, Section 123D rectifications).
2. **Neutral Dispute Preparation (`propertyTaxDisputeGuideService.js`)**:
   - Outlines the 30-day objection limitation period.
   - Provides an evidence checklist (sanction plans, previous 3-year receipts, ARO representation).
3. **Receipt Retention Safeguards**:
   - Best practices for saving digital municipal counterfoils and UTR transaction codes to prove zero arrears during property transfers.
4. **Glossary & Anti-Calculation Mandate**:
   - Defines Unit Area Value, Rateable Value, and SAS.
   - Prohibits calculating binding tax figures from incomplete data.
