# Legal Heir and Surviving Member Certificate Guide (India)

## Statutory & Administrative Context
- Issued by the executive revenue administration: **Tahsildar / Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO) / Revenue Divisional Officer (RDO) / Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM)** under state-specific executive revenue orders.
- Governed by respective personal succession laws (e.g. **Hindu Succession Act, 1956**, **Indian Succession Act, 1925**) for defining Class-I / immediate legal heirs.

## Critical Distinction: Revenue Legal Heir Certificate vs. Court Succession Certificate
1. **Revenue Legal Heir / Surviving Member Certificate (SMC)**:
   - Administrative certification of surviving immediate family members (spouse, children, parents).
   - Issued primarily for:
     - Claiming deceased employee pensions, gratuity, and terminal benefits.
     - Compassionate government employment.
     - Transfer of domestic utilities (electricity, water, LPG) and property tax registry.
     - Settlement of small provident fund / insurance claims where nominee is absent.
   - **Limitation**: Not a judicial decree of title or succession. Does not conclusively adjudicate high-value disputed financial assets or transfer ownership in contentious estates.
2. **Succession Certificate (Issued by Civil Court under Indian Succession Act)**:
   - Formal judicial decree issued by a District Judge / Civil Judge Senior Division.
   - Required by scheduled banks, mutual funds, and stock registrars for release of substantial financial securities, shares, debts, and deposits where title is contested or value exceeds statutory institutional thresholds.

## Class-I Legal Heirs Recognized
- Wife / Husband (Surviving Spouse)
- Sons and Daughters (including married daughters and legally adopted children)
- Mother of the deceased (Note: Father is a Class-II heir under Hindu Succession Act, but some states like Tamil Nadu under revised 2022 rules allow legitimate Class-II heir certification when no Class-I heir exists).
