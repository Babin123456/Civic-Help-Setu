# Disability Certificate and UDID Application Journey Guide — Research Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#509 — Add a Disability Certificate and UDID application journey guide](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/509)  
**Scope:** Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016, 21 recognized disability categories, Unique Disability ID (UDID) portal by DEPwD (Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities), designated Medical Board assessment, and disability percentage thresholds.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** A Disability Certificate / UDID card is an official statutory identity document issued under the RPwD Act, 2016 verifying the existence, nature, and percentage of benchmark disability (≥ 40%).
- **What It Proves:**
  - Eligibility for statutory 4% reservations in government recruitment and 5% in higher educational institutions under the RPwD Act.
  - Entitlement to disability pensions, railway concession passes, subsidized assistive aids (ADIP scheme), and income tax deductions (Sections 80U / 80DD).
- **What It Does NOT Do:**
  - It does NOT predict medical treatment outcomes or provide instant disability percentages online. Assessment can only be performed by a designated government Medical Board.
  - A permanent certificate is distinct from a temporary/validity-period certificate (for children or conditions subject to clinical variation).

---

## 2. Who Can Apply & 21 Recognized Disabilities

Under the RPwD Act, 2016, 21 categories of disabilities are statutorily recognized, including:
1. Locomotor Disability, Leprosy Cured persons, Cerebral Palsy, Dwarfism, Muscular Dystrophy, Acid Attack victims.
2. Blindness, Low Vision.
3. Deaf, Hard of Hearing.
4. Speech and Language disability.
5. Intellectual Disability, Specific Learning Disabilities, Autism Spectrum Disorder.
6. Mental Illness.
7. Multiple Sclerosis, Parkinson's disease.
8. Hemophilia, Thalassemia, Sickle Cell Disease.
9. Multiple Disabilities including deaf-blindness.

## 3. Medical Board Assessment & Application Journey

1. **Online Registration:** File application on the official Swavlamban Portal (`swavlambancard.gov.in`).
2. **Hospital Allocation:** Portal assigns the designated District Hospital / Chief Medical Officer (CMO) board based on applicant's current or permanent district.
3. **Physical Assessment Appointment:** Applicant visits the designated hospital on the scheduled date for clinical evaluation by medical specialists.
4. **Assessment & Percentage Determination:** The Medical Board tests mobility, vision, hearing, or psychological criteria and records the disability percentage on the central portal.
5. **e-UDID & Card Dispatch:** Digitally signed disability certificate and e-UDID card are generated immediately for download; physical laminated card is dispatched by post.

---

## 4. Key Dates & Timeline Distinctions

- **Application Date:** When the online form is registered and acknowledgement number is issued.
- **Medical Assessment Date:** When the candidate appears before the Medical Board.
- **Certificate Issue Date:** When the CMO / Medical Superintendent signs the certificate.
- **Reassessment / Validity Date:** If the disability is temporary (e.g. progressive or pediatric), a reassessment date is explicitly printed; permanent conditions carry permanent validity.
