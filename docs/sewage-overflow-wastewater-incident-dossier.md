# Sewage Overflow and Wastewater Discharge Incident Dossier

## Purpose
Builds a structured incident dossier for documenting sewer backups, dangerous open manholes, and illicit industrial wastewater discharges, ensuring verified evidence can be routed directly to municipal drainage engineers.

## Key Features
- **Safety Hazard Detection**: Automatically marks incidents involving open manholes or exposed wiring as Emergency Hazards with explicit safety warnings.
- **Evidence Chronology**: Tracks sequential observations over time to document municipal delays.
- **Agency Escalation Packets**: Generates formatted formal complaint dossiers for state water supply and sewerage boards.
