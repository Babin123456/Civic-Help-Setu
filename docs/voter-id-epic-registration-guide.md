# Voter ID (EPIC) Registration and Correction Guide — Research Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#512 — Add a Voter ID (EPIC) registration and correction guide](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/512)  
**Scope:** Representation of the People Act, 1950, Election Commission of India (ECI) Voter Services Portal (`voters.eci.gov.in`), Form 6 (new voter), Form 7 (objection/deletion), Form 8 (shifting/correction/replacement), Booth Level Officer (BLO) field verification, and electoral roll inclusion milestones.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** An Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC) is an official identity card issued by the Election Commission of India under the authority of the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO). It certifies inclusion in the electoral roll and grants constitutional franchise to vote in parliamentary and assembly elections.
- **What It Proves:**
  - Certified identity and registered voter status in a specific parliamentary/assembly constituency.
  - Recognized across India as official proof of identity and address.
- **What It Does NOT Prove:**
  - Having a physical card alone does not entitle you to vote if your name is **deleted from the live electoral roll**. The entry in the active voter list is the sole legal basis for voting.

---

## 2. Four Annual Qualifying Dates (Amended ECI Rules)

Under the amended election laws, citizens do not have to wait until 1 January to become eligible. ECI provides **four qualifying dates** in a calendar year:
1. **1st January**
2. **1st April**
3. **1st July**
4. **1st October**
Citizens turning 18 can submit advance applications up to 3 months prior to each qualifying date.

---

## 3. Statutory ECI Forms & Application Pathways

1. **Form 6:** Application for new voter registration / enrollment of Indian citizens turning 18 or first-time electors.
2. **Form 6A:** Application for inclusion of name in electoral roll by an Overseas (NRI) elector.
3. **Form 7:** Application for objection to inclusion or seeking deletion of name from electoral roll (e.g. death or permanent relocation).
4. **Form 8:** Single multi-purpose form for:
   - Shifting of residence (within or outside assembly constituency);
   - Correction of entries (name, age, relative name, photo, gender);
   - Issue of replacement EPIC without alteration (for lost or mutilated cards);
   - Marking of Person with Disability (PwD) for accessible polling assistance.

---

## 4. Application Milestones & Verification Journey

1. **Online Filing:** Submit application on `voters.eci.gov.in` or via Voter Helpline App. Receive a unique 12-digit Application Reference Number.
2. **Field Verification:** Booth Level Officer (BLO) visits the residential premises to physically verify the applicant's presence, age proof, and address proof.
3. **Scrutiny & ERO Order:** The Electoral Registration Officer / Assistant Electoral Registration Officer reviews the BLO report and enters the name into the draft electoral roll.
4. **Publication & EPIC Dispatch:** Once included in the live voter list, the voter can immediately download the digital e-EPIC. The physical laminated card is printed and delivered free of charge by Department of Posts.
