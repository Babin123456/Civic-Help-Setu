# SC/ST Caste Certificate Application Guide — Research & Implementation Notes

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Related issue:** [#504 — Add an SC/ST Caste Certificate application guide](https://github.com/GoldRay19/Civic-Help-Setu/issues/504)  
**Scope:** Statutory issuance of Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) certificates under Presidential Orders (Articles 341 and 342 of the Constitution of India), Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment / Ministry of Tribal Affairs guidelines, and state revenue department verification rules.  
**Status:** Citizen-facing research notes and structured data contract implementation.

---

## 1. What This Document Is and Is Not

- **Purpose & Legal Proof:** An SC/ST Certificate is an official certificate issued by a competent revenue authority verifying that the holder belongs to a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe specified in the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order or Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order for the relevant State/UT.
- **What It Does NOT Prove:**
  - It does not automatically guarantee admission, recruitment, or welfare disbursements—it only proves caste status for reservation eligibility.
  - Caste cannot be inferred from a profile, name, or occupation; it requires formal statutory determination.
  - An SC/ST certificate is distinct from an OBC or EWS certificate (which include income/creamy-layer criteria). SC/ST certificates do not expire and have lifetime validity unless cancelled due to misrepresentation or fraud.

---

## 2. Competent Issuing Authorities in India

As per Ministry of Home Affairs / Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment guidelines:
1. District Magistrate / Additional District Magistrate / Collector / Deputy Commissioner.
2. Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Taluk Magistrate / Executive Magistrate.
3. Revenue Officer not below the rank of Tehsildar (in many states, the designated competent authority).
4. Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) of the area where the candidate and/or family normally resides.

---

## 3. Ordinary Residence Rule & Cut-off Dates

- **State/UT of Origin:** A person belongs to an SC or ST in relation to the State or Union Territory in which their forefathers resided at the date of the Presidential Notification (the standard cut-off year being **1950 for SC** and **1950 for ST**, or the date when the territory became part of India).
- **Migration Cases:** If a family migrated to another state after the Presidential Notification, they receive an SC/ST certificate based on the parental origin state (they remain entitled to central benefits in relation to their home state list, but generally not to state-specific benefits of the host state).

---

## 4. Required Evidence & Document Checklist

### A. Essential Documents
- Proof of relationship and lineage: Father's, mother's, or direct paternal ancestor's verified SC/ST Certificate, School Leaving Certificate, or Land Record (showing caste).
- Proof of residence: Pre-cut-off year land deeds, khatiyan, revenue records, or continuous voter list extracts.
- Identity and age proof of applicant: Birth Certificate, 10th marksheet, or Aadhaar card.
- Self-declaration affidavit sworn before an Executive Magistrate or Notary Public.

### B. Verification Process
- Field enquiry by Village Administrative Officer (VAO) / Revenue Inspector (RI).
- Verification of ancestral residence and community standing.
- Recommendation by local enquiry committee (where mandated by state law).
