# Plain-Language Guide to Municipal Budgets and Ward Spending

## Overview
Municipal budget documents are often filled with bureaucratic jargon, preventing ordinary citizens from understanding how public tax money is allocated and spent in their neighbourhoods. This service provides a citizen-friendly educational guide, glossary, and ward-level budget calculator.

## Key Components
1. **Plain-Language Glossary**: Clear definitions and real-world examples for terms like CapEx, OpEx, Tenders, and Ward Committee Allocations.
2. **Budget Lifecycle**: 5 sequential stages from budget preparation and council debate to tendering, execution, and social audits.
3. **Ward Budget & Spending Analysis**: Breakdown of ward allocations by category (footpaths, drainage, parks) and calculation of utilization rates.
4. **Actionable Civic Guidance**: Step-by-step instructions for citizens on participating in ward committee meetings and tracking civic work orders.
