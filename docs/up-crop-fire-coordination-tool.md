# UP Crop-Residue Fire Incident Reporting & Response Coordination Tool

## Overview
Post-harvest paddy stubble burning (*Parali Dahan*) during October–November leads to severe atmospheric particulate pollution (PM2.5 / PM10) across Uttar Pradesh and the National Capital Region (NCR).

Enforced by the **Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)**, the **UP Pollution Control Board (UPPCB)**, and the **Department of Agriculture (`upagriculture.com`)**, proactive fire detection and farmer-support mechanisms are critical.

This service coordinates citizen observations with rapid emergency triage and sustainable Crop Residue Management (CRM) solutions.

---

## Safety Emergency Triage vs Standard Monitoring

### 1. High-Priority Life-Safety Emergencies
If an active stubble fire satisfies any of the following criteria, the system flags a **Critical Emergency** requiring immediate call routing to **112 / Fire 101**:
- Proximity to residential settlements, thatched huts, or dairy sheds.
- Threat to harvest grain storage godowns or petrol pumps.
- Heavy smoke drifting across state highways causing zero-visibility pileup risks.
- Proximity to 132/220/400 kV high-tension electrical transmission lines.

### 2. Standard Environmental Monitoring
- Isolated, contained field burning is routed for ground verification by the local revenue accountant (*Lekhpal*), Kisan Sahayak, and Gram Pradhan.

---

## Geospatial Deduplication & Clustering
To prevent multiple passing motorists or villagers from triggering separate dispatches for the same fire plume:
- Reports occurring within **1.5 km radial distance** and within **3 hours** of an existing open incident are automatically consolidated under a single Cluster Leader ID.

---

## In-situ & Ex-situ Crop Residue Management (CRM) Subsidies

The UP Government provides heavy capital subsidies to discourage burning:
1. **Super Seeder**: Sows wheat seeds directly into standing paddy stubble (50% individual subsidy up to ₹1,05,000; 80% for Custom Hiring Centres - CHC).
2. **Happy Seeder**: Direct-drilling seed drill machine (50% subsidy up to ₹75,000).
3. **Straw Baler & Rake**: Compresses straw into commercial industrial bales for biomass power plants (50% subsidy up to ₹2,00,000).
4. **Pusa Bio-Decomposer Spray**: Free microbial capsule distribution through Block Development Offices.

## Escalation Matrix & Helpline Contacts
- **Fire & Rescue Emergency**: Dial **101**.
- **Police Emergency Response**: Dial **112**.
- **Kisan Call Centre Advisory**: Toll-Free **1800-180-1551**.
- **Administrative Enforcement**: Lekhpal / Gram Pradhan -> Agriculture Technical Assistant -> SDM / Tehsildar -> District Agriculture Officer (DAO).


## Environmental Penalties & Adverse Khatauni Red Entries
Under orders from the Supreme Court, National Green Tribunal (NGT), and CAQM:
- **Small Farmers (<2 Acres)**: Environmental compensation fine of ₹2,500 per incident.
- **Medium Farmers (2–5 Acres)**: Fine of ₹5,000 per incident.
- **Large Farmers (>5 Acres)**: Fine of ₹15,000 per incident.
- **Red Entry in Khatauni (Laal Indraj)**: An administrative entry is recorded in the farmer's land records, disqualifying the holding from PM-KISAN installment credits and agricultural cooperative subsidies for two cropping seasons.
- The Civic Help Setu framework emphasizes early machinery mobilization and bio-decomposer awareness to prevent farmers from falling into these punitive penalties.


- Continuous coordination between agriculture field functionaries and village communities prevents crop fires before they spread.
