# Footpath Continuity and Safe Walking Route Audit

## Purpose
Provides a route-level usability and walkability audit enabling citizens to document broken pedestrian corridors, abrupt level changes, missing sidewalks, and blocked ramps.

## Key Features
- **Route Continuity Scoring**: Evaluates trips from origin to destination across general walking, wheelchair, and stroller profiles.
- **Defect Mapping**: Flags pedestrian bottlenecks across specific corridors without requiring proprietary map software.
- **Official Export Dossier**: Generates summary packets ready for submission to local public works departments (PWD) or municipal ward engineers.
