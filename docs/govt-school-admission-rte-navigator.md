# Government School Admission and RTE 12(1)(c) Application Navigator

## Statutory Framework
The **Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009** guarantees the fundamental right to education for children between the ages of 6 and 14 years under Article 21A of the Constitution of India.

### Key Statutory Provisions
1. **Section 3(1) - Right to Free Education**: Every child aged 6 to 14 has the right to free and compulsory elementary education in a neighbourhood school.
2. **Section 12(1)(c) - 25% Private School Quota**: Mandates that private unaided schools admit at least 25% of children from Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and Disadvantaged Groups (DG) in entry-level classes (Pre-school / Grade 1) without charging tuition or allied fees.
3. **Section 14 - Age Proof Safeguard**: No child shall be denied admission to school for lack of age proof. A municipal birth certificate, hospital record, Anganwadi record, or parent affidavit is legally admissible.
4. **Section 13 - No Capitation Fee or Screening**: Forbids schools from collecting capitation fees or subjecting children/parents to interviews or screening procedures.

## Admission Channels

### 1. State Government & Municipal Schools
- Open to all residents within the municipal or panchayat ward.
- Entitlements: Zero tuition fee, free uniforms, free NCERT/state textbooks, and Mid-Day Meal (PM POSHAN) coverage.
- Continuous admission cycle: Admissions cannot be refused even if applying mid-academic session.

### 2. RTE Section 12(1)(c) Private School Quota (25%)
- Administered via state-level online centralized lottery portals (e.g. RTE Maharashtra, RTE Karnataka, RTE Rajasthan, Delhi Edudel).
- Free education up to Grade 8; state reimburses per-child expenditure to private institutions.
- Priority distance catchment bands:
  - **Tier 1 (0–1 km):** Walking distance — highest priority in lottery algorithms.
  - **Tier 2 (1–3 km):** Neighbourhood circle — second preference.
  - **Tier 3 (3–6 km):** Outer radius — allotted if seats remain vacant.

---

## Eligibility Groups under RTE 12(1)(c)
- **Economically Weaker Section (EWS)**: Annual household income below notified state threshold (typically ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,50,000/year). Requires valid Tehsildar/SDM income certificate.
- **Disadvantaged Group (DG)**:
  - Scheduled Castes (SC) & Scheduled Tribes (ST)
  - Other Backward Classes (OBC - Non-Creamy Layer)
  - Children with Special Needs (CWSN / 40%+ disability certificate)
  - Orphans, transgender children, and children of HIV-affected parents.

---

## Redressal & Escalation
If a school refuses admission, charges hidden fees, or discriminates against an RTE beneficiary:
1. File complaint with the **Block Education Officer (BEO)** / District Education Officer (DEO).
2. Escalate to the **State Commission for Protection of Child Rights (SCPCR)** or **NCPCR**.
