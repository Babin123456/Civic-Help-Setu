# UP Public Cremation and Burial Ground Facility Information and Maintenance Directory

## Overview
Under the **Uttar Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1959**, the **UP Municipalities Act, 1916**, and the rural **Antyeshti Sthal Vikas Yojana** (Panchayati Raj Department), every urban local body and rural block is mandated to provide dignified, sanitary, and well-maintained public cremation and burial grounds (*Shamshan Ghat, Mokshadham, Kabristan, Qabristan, Christian Cemeteries*).

During times of deep personal bereavement, citizens frequently encounter dilapidated waiting sheds, broken handpumps, non-functional high-mast lights at night, and illegal fee extortion by private middlemen.

This service catalogs public facility infrastructure, transparent municipal fee schedules, and direct civic maintenance escalation pathways.

---

## Core Civil Amenity Norms

1. **Running Water Supply**: Dedicated water tank with multiple brass taps or high-discharge submersible pump for mandatory post-cremation bathing and ablution rites.
2. **Covered Waiting Sheds (Varandah)**: Clean, shaded shelters protecting funeral attendees from heavy rain, heatwaves, or winter cold.
3. **High-Mast Night Illumination**: Dedicated solar or electric mast lighting ensuring round-the-clock safety and visibility.
4. **All-Weather Paved Access**: Concrete/bituminous access roads connecting the main highway to the ghat entrance to facilitate hearse vehicles.
5. **Boundary Walls & Stray Animal Barriers**: Gated perimeter walls preventing animal ingress and preserving the dignity of the sacred grounds.

---

## Fee Transparency & Extortion Prevention

- **Statutory Municipal Rates**:
  - Electric / CNG cremation charges are fixed by the Municipal Corporation board (typically ₹500 to ₹1,000 for non-BPL families, and completely free for BPL / destitute citizens).
- **Mandatory Display Board**:
  - The law mandates an official display board listing authorized rates, wood weight costs, and municipal contact helplines at the entrance gate.
- **Reporting Middleman Extortion**: Overcharging can be escalated directly to the Municipal Health Officer or via Civic Helpline **1533**.

---

## Strict Bereavement Dignity & Privacy Safeguards
- **Zero Deceased Names**: The platform does NOT collect or record names of deceased persons, family relations, or causes of death.
- **Zero Ritual / Facial Imagery**: No photographs of funeral ceremonies, grieving mourners, or pyres are permitted.
- The system focuses exclusively on public civil infrastructure, maintenance logs, and official municipal accountability.


## Citizen Assistance & Public Hearing Workflow
1. **Notice Verification**: Always compare demanded wood or CNG fees against the official Municipal Rate Board at the entrance.
2. **Immediate Redressal**: In case of illegal lockouts or extortion, contact the on-duty Sanitary Inspector or the municipal 1533 helpline immediately.
3. **Panchayat Representation**: For rural crematoria (Antyeshti Sthal) requiring urgent repair of solar lamps or water pipes, representations should be submitted to the Gram Vikas Adhikari (Secretary) or during Block Tehsil Diwas.
4. **Cleanliness & Ash Management**: Citizens can flag non-cleared ash or unhygienic conditions to promote eco-friendly pyre practices.
